Six steps

Here's exactly what happens, in order.

You are part of every decision. Nothing moves forward until you're happy.

1

Discover your perfect solution With Tom and his team

Your consultation. We sit down, listen to what you've been through, look at what's going on, and talk through your options honestly. No pressure, no sales pitch.

2

Explore your sedation and implant options With Dr. Silver

Meet your implant dentist. Together you'll go over your sedation choices, so you can be as relaxed or as fully asleep as you'd like, and exactly how and where your implants will be placed.

3

Build your dream smile together With Tom

You're part of the design and fabrication. Bring photos of smiles you love, your own from years ago or ones you admire. Tom designs your teeth with you, and you see the results before anything is final.

4

Choose your path: Teeth-in-a-Day or Conventional You decide

Both paths end with the same strong, permanent teeth. The difference is what you wear while your implants heal.

Option A · Teeth-in-a-Day

Walk out with teeth that don't come out.

Your implants are placed and a temporary row of teeth is fastened to them in the same appointment, same day. You leave with fixed teeth while everything heals.

Same appointment · Same day
Option B · Conventional

Heal first, then your permanent teeth.

Wear a denture for about four months while the implants fully integrate and heal. Then we make your perfect row of teeth that do not come out.

About 4 months healing
5

Your final teeth

Once your gums have settled and the implants have completely healed, your strong, finalized row of teeth is installed. Adjustments can still be made before they're completed, so you're totally satisfied.

6

Follow-up, maintenance and patient care

We do the work of setting up your regular hygiene visits and doctor visits, so your implants and teeth stay healthy, happy and clean, and last as long as possible.
