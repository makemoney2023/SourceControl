# Gemini Omni city-flight baseline — 2026-09-02

## What landed

All ten placeholder city legs were replaced with Gemini Omni footage. Each leg starts from the previous leg's encoded desktop final frame; the first leg starts from the approved Era plate. Desktop files are 1920×1080 with GOP 8, mobile files are 720×1280 with GOP 4, and posters come from frame 0 of the encoded desktop files.

The 10-second skyline peak uses the eight-second Omni motion slowed to ten seconds. This preserves moving footage for the full peak instead of freezing the final two seconds.

## Verification

- `npm run verify:city-assets`: all 10 legs verified.
- Runner contract tests: 5 passed.
- Midpoint stills from every encoded desktop leg were reviewed.
- Legs 7–10 were rerolled together after the first peak stayed near street level and the first final hold contained portrait billboards.
- Legs 9–10 were rerolled together, then leg 10 once more, to remove portrait-led advertising and restore the aerial close.
- Accepted footage is photographic and product-free. Some generated distant light panels contain indistinct pseudo-lettering, but the reviewed frames contain no branded product presentation.
