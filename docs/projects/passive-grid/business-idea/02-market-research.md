## Executive summary

Passive-grid targets a narrow but growing wedge inside the global atmospheric water generator (AWG) market: **sorbent-based, grid-tolerant water harvesting** for off-grid homeowners, cottage owners, and preparedness buyers who cannot rely on refrigeration AWGs below ~30% RH or municipal supply during outages.

**Market size (triangulated):**

| Layer | Estimate (2026) | Confidence |
|-------|-----------------|------------|
| **TAM** — Global residential + off-grid AWG | **USD 450M–900M** | Low–Medium |
| **SAM** — Canada off-grid / cottage / preparedness AWG buyers | **USD 35M–120M** | Low |
| **SOM** — Ontario bootstrapped launch (Years 1–3) | **USD 0.1M–0.8M** (50–400 units) | Medium |

The global AWG category is large and growing (consensus **USD 2.9B–3.7B in 2026**, CAGR **6.5%–10%**), but **cooling-condensation products dominate >99% of revenue** ([Precedence Research](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/atmospheric-water-generator-market)). Wet-desiccation / sorbent AWG is a **nascent sub-segment** (~1% of category today, faster growth). Passive-grid's differentiation — zeolite/MOF sorbent cycles, optional Pi edge control, and a zero-power passive variant — maps to buyers who prioritize **humidity-range performance, grid-down resilience, and self-sufficiency narrative** over lowest cost-per-litre.

**Primary beachhead:** Ontario cottage country and rural off-grid households (~400K+ recreational properties nationally; Ontario is the largest provincial cluster). Secondary: remote communities and disaster-preparedness buyers.

**Assumption A5 (premium for self-sufficiency):** **Partially supported, segment-dependent.** Buyers already spend **USD 25K–80K** on off-grid solar ([VoltFlow](https://www.voltflow.net/blog/off-grid-solar-canada-2026)) and **USD 799–1,770** on entry refrigeration AWGs ([EcoloBlue](https://www.ecoloblue.com/en/6-atmospheric-water-generators)). A **USD 1,200–2,500** sorbent unit is plausible for resilience-motivated buyers but **not** for municipal-water households optimizing cost-per-litre ([EnergyBS](https://energybs.com/green-living/water/atmospheric-water-generator-awg-hydropanel-guide/)).

**Recommendation for Phase 3:** Validate SAM with primary interviews (10–15 Ontario cottage/off-grid owners), benchmark yield vs. zeolite prototype, and pressure-test ASP against EcoloBlue and bottled-water hauling costs in Muskoka/Kawartha.

## TAM / SAM / SOM

### Methodology

Three approaches were triangulated:

1. **Top-down** — Apply residential/off-grid share of global AWG reports to wet-desiccation segment.
2. **Regional** — Derive Canada/Ontario from North America AWG estimates.
3. **Bottom-up** — Household counts × adoption rate × assumed ASP.

Confidence is **Low** for dollar TAM/SAM (industry reports aggregate all AWG types; sorbent segment not broken out consistently). **Medium** for SOM (founder-controlled GTM assumptions).

## Customer segments

### Primary — Off-grid / preparedness homeowners (Ontario)

**Who:** Rural property owners with solar/generator backup, well or lake intake, or no reliable municipal tie-in. Often already invested in resilience stack (solar, battery, generator, food storage).

**Size signal:** Off-grid and remote homes = **5%–8%** of Canadian residential storage installs ([IndexBox](https://www.indexbox.io/store/canada-residential-solar-energy-storage-market-analysis-forecast-size-trends-and-insights/)); Ontario off-grid clusters in Muskoka, Haliburton, Parry Sound, Northern Ontario ([VoltFlow](https://www.voltflow.net/blog/off-grid-solar-canada-2026)).

**Need:** Supplemental potable water when wells run low, power is out, or hauling/bottled water is costly.

**Buy trigger:** Drought advisory, well failure, ice-storm outage, or "self-sufficiency" project milestone.

---

### Primary — Cottage / recreational property owners (Ontario)

**Who:** Seasonal or year-round cottage owners; median Ontario recreational single-family **~CAD 631K** (2025) ([Royal LePage](https://www.therecord.com/business/real-estate/more-americans-are-looking-at-ontario-cottages-but-market-remains-in-a-holding-pattern/article_f0a78bfc-7d59-5121-89ac-4cb462daf919.html)). **59%** want year-round use ([Royal LePage / Yahoo](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/canadians-turning-recreational-properties-both-080000182.html)).

**Size signal:** **>1.2M** Canadian secondary-residence households; Ontario largest provincial market.

**Need:** Drinking water independent of seasonal well variability; avoid plastic bottled water; enable extended stays.

**Buy trigger:** Renovation, year-round conversion, low-water advisory (e.g. eastern Ontario 2025 drought — [CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/eastern-ontario-conservation-authorities-water-conservation-drought-conditions-1.7602368)).

---

### Secondary — Remote communities & institutional resilience

**Who:** Remote Indigenous communities off diesel grid (**280+** communities per [Aurora Generators / rural grid reporting](https://auroragenerators.com/blog/rural-canada-power-grid-challenges/)); municipal emergency management; eco-lodges.

**Need:** Decentralized potable water; reduce bottled-water logistics.

**Buy trigger:** Federal/provincial water-security funding; disaster response procurement.

**Note:** Long sales cycles, certification burden — **Phase 2+** segment, not Year 1 beachhead.

---

### Secondary — Disaster preparedness / emergency management

**Who:** Households expanding prep kits post-wildfire/flood seasons; **44%** of Canadians want *more* emergency-preparedness funding ([Nanos](https://nanos.co/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/2024-2678-DMG-Aug-Populated-report-updated.pdf)).

**Size signal:** Canada incident/emergency management market **USD 7.23B** (2025) ([MRFR](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/canada-incident-emergency-management-market-60060)) — mostly services/software; hardware is small slice.

**Need:** Grid-down water source when stored water depletes.

## Porter's five forces (summary)

| Force | Intensity | Rationale |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| **Threat of new entrants** | **Medium–High** | AWG market fragmented; 3D printing + Pi lowers hardware barrier; MOF IP concentrated in academia ([PatSnap](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/rd-blog/atmospheric-water-harvesting-2026-patsnap-eureka/)) |
| **Supplier power** | **Medium** | Zeolite/silica commoditized; MOF materials specialized; UV/filter suppliers numerous (internal BOM) |
| **Buyer power** | **Medium** | Niche buyers compare to bottled water, wells, Berkey, EcoloBlue; high education burden |
| **Threat of substitutes** | **High** | Municipal water (cheap in Ontario), bottled water, rainwater, wells, refrigeration AWGs, SOURCE hydropanels |
| **Competitive rivalry** | **Medium** | Established AWG brands (Watergen, EcoloBlue, SOURCE) dominate mindshare; wet-desiccation niche less crowded |

**Strategic implication:** Compete on **humidity range + grid-down passivity + localized smart control**, not on cost-per-litre vs. tap water.

## Key market trends

1. **Global AWG growth (6.5%–10% CAGR)** driven by scarcity and decentralization ([Precedence](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/atmospheric-water-generator-market), [Fortune BI](https://www.fortunebusinessinsights.com/atmospheric-water-generator-market-103321)).

2. **Sorbent / MOF AWH moving from lab to deployable devices** — solar-thermal desorption, composite MOF-salt gels, structured sorbents ([PatSnap 2026](https://www.patsnap.com/resources/blog/rd-blog/atmospheric-water-harvesting-2026-patsnap-eureka/)). Validates long-term MOF strategy (A4).

3. **Cooling condensation still >99% share** — passive-grid must clearly articulate why sorbent path matters ([Precedence](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/atmospheric-water-generator-market)).

4. **Climate resilience and drought in Ontario** — 2025 eastern Ontario drought, well failures, conservation advisories ([CBC](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/ottawa/eastern-ontario-conservation-authorities-water-conservation-drought-conditions-1.7602368), [Canada1Water/Kingstonist](https://www.canada1water.ca/blog/kingstonistnews-failing-fields-dry-wells-eastern-ontarios-summer-of-drought)).

5. **Off-grid investment sustained** — Ontario cottage-country solar + storage; buyers view off-grid as asset ([VoltFlow](https://www.voltflow.net/blog/off-grid-solar-canada-2026)).

6. **Smart AWG / IoT integration** — industry reports cite IoT scheduling and efficiency gains ([Precedence](https://www.precedenceresearch.com/atmospheric-water-generator-market)); supports Pi edge-control variant.

7. **Canada NA AWG growth outpacing US in some forecasts** — Canada "fastest-growing" in North America ([MRFR NA AWG](https://www.marketresearchfuture.com/reports/north-america-atmospheric-water-generator-market-47899)).

## Open questions for Phase 3

1. **Primary research:** Interview 10–15 Ontario cottage/off-grid owners — validate pain ranking, ASP, and maintenance tolerance.
2. **Yield proof:** What L/day does zeolite MVP produce at Ontario summer vs. winter RH? Required for credible avatar messaging.
3. **Product line:** Lead with passive grid-down unit, smart Pi unit, or single hybrid SKU?
4. **Regulatory:** Health Canada / NSF path and timeline for "drinking water appliance" claims?
5. **Competitive intel:** Deep dive on SOURCE, Watergen residential, and any Canadian wet-desiccation entrants (peer: `competitive-intelligence-analyst`).
6. **Channel:** DTC online vs. cottage-country installer partnerships vs. prepper retail — CAC by channel?
7. **MOF timeline:** Supplier landscape and cost curve for production MOF (supports long-term moat).
8. **Indigenous remote pilot:** Feasibility and funding programs (ISC, Clean Energy for Rural and Remote Communities)?
9. **Operating cost narrative:** kWh/L vs. passive solar-thermal — which story wins in Ontario humidity band?
10. **Danny's role:** GTM / hardware split affects SOM execution (from Phase 1 open item).
