## Strategic thesis

Serious ADRK-aligned Rottweiler buyers treat purchase as **due diligence**. Blacksage's public presence must earn that trust through proof and standards-informed education, then convert **qualified** interest. Category evidence (Phase 2) and v1 failure mode E show that inverting this order — spectacle or apply before proof — fails.

## Positioning and ICP

### Positioning statement

For **serious Rottweiler buyers** who research health, structure, and program integrity before a 10+ year commitment, **Blacksage Kennels** is a **German / ADRK-aligned breeding program** whose public presence leads with **verifiable evidence and standards-informed education** — not spectacle or instant checkout. Unlike volume kennels or polished sites without proof, Blacksage earns trust through **evidence density** and invites **qualified inquiry only after** the buyer can evaluate the program.

*Kind: Inference for kennel-specific proof pending operator Tier 2 facts; Fact for category buyer behavior.*

### ICP

| Segment | Who | Job |
|---------|-----|-----|
| **Primary** | Serious ADRK-aligned buyers (family companion, sport/working, import/pedigree sub-segments) | Due diligence before commitment |
| **Secondary** | Referrers (trainers, clubs, prior owners) | Share URL without reputational risk |
| **Anti-persona** | Impulse shoppers, guard-dog fantasy, price-only, checkout-expecters | Filter out |

Full detail: [`.agents/product-marketing.md`](.agents/product-marketing.md)

### Messaging pillars

1. Standards-aligned type and structure (ADRK/FCI No. 147; natural tail; no giant tropes)  
2. Temperament within ADRK bounds (devoted, even-tempered, biddable — no aggression marketing)  
3. Verifiable health transparency (categories now; per-dog links when operator inventory exists)  
4. Deliberate placement, not volume (selective; no FOMO scarcity)  
5. Education before sale  

### CTA language

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Begin your inquiry / Submit inquiry | Apply now / Buy / Shop / Reserve |
| Interest list / waitlist (when Q1 clear) | Limited time / Only X left |
