# Product Marketing Context — Blacksage Kennels

*Last updated: 2026-07-27 · Phase 3 Strategy · product-marketing-manager*

> **Strategy lock:** Trust-first (D2) — education and verifiable health/pedigree signals **before** qualified inquiry. Evidence density beats visual spectacle. Apply-first (D3) and cosmetic rebuild (D7) are rejected.

---

## Product overview

### One-liner

**Blacksage Kennels** is a German / ADRK-aligned Rottweiler breeding program whose public web channel helps serious buyers **evaluate program credibility** before initiating a qualified inquiry.

### What we offer

| Layer | Definition | Kind |
|-------|------------|------|
| **Core service** | Selective Rottweiler breeding aligned with ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147 — structure, temperament, and health-informed pairing | **Fact** (breed focus per intake) |
| **Web channel role** | Credibility and evaluation surface — not a storefront, not a spectacle experience | **Inference** (Phase 2 evidence) |
| **Conversion outcome** | Qualified inquiry / interest or waitlist registration **after** trust content | **Decision** (D2 trust-first) |

### Product category

Premium ethical dog breeder — **service business** with a marketing/conversion website as primary research channel. Buyers compare Blacksage to other ADRK/German-line kennels during due diligence, not to pet retailers or puppy marketplaces.

### Business model (category context — not Blacksage pricing)

| Element | Category norm | Blacksage status |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| Puppy pricing | ~$1,500–$2,500 mainstream ethical; $3k–$7k+ premium/import-adjacent; **0/8 competitors publish prices on site** | **Unknown — do not invent** |
| Waitlists | 6–12+ months common; deposit after trust established | **Unknown — depends on Q1** |
| Revenue model | Selective placements, not volume | **Assumption** (aligned with positioning) |

## Positioning

### Positioning statement

For **serious Rottweiler buyers** who research health, structure, and program integrity before committing to a 10+ year companion, **Blacksage Kennels** is a **German / ADRK-aligned breeding program** whose public presence leads with **verifiable evidence and standards-informed education** — not spectacle or instant checkout. Unlike volume kennels or visually polished sites without proof, Blacksage earns trust through **evidence density** (named dogs, health transparency, temperament narrative within ADRK bounds, deliberate placement process) and invites **qualified inquiry only after** the buyer can evaluate the program.

**Kind:** **Inference** — strategic synthesis from Phase 2 evidence; kennel-specific proof pending operator input.

### Category frame

| Frame | Statement | Kind |
|-------|-----------|------|
| **We compete on** | Proof density, standards literacy, placement integrity | **Inference** (8/8 competitor pattern) |
| **We do not compete on** | Scroll 3D, cinematic UX, price transparency, instant availability | **Fact** (0/8 competitors use 3D; 0/8 publish prices) |
| **Prestige definition** | In this category, prestige = **evidence density**, not design novelty | **Inference** (Phase 2 CI) |

### Differentiation vs. category

| Dimension | Category norm (premium kennels) | Blacksage direction | v1 gap |
|-----------|--------------------------------|---------------------|--------|
| Trust signals | Named parents, OFA/CHIC, pedigrees, education, contracts | **Lead with same proof hierarchy** | Placeholder content; no evidence layer |
| Visual UX | Dated templates tolerated when proof-rich | Clean, credible IA — **proof over polish** | 3D spectacle without authentic media |

| Temperament narrative | ADRK bounds: good-natured, devoted, even-tempered | No aggression/guard-dog hype | Partially aligned in stub voice |

## Messaging pillars

Five pillars grounded in ADRK/FCI temperament bounds and Phase 2 trust-signal ranking. Each pillar includes voice bounds and proof requirements.

### Pillar 1 — Standards-aligned type and structure

**Message:** Blacksage breeds toward ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147 — correct proportions, clearly defined rich tan markings, compact powerful build, natural tail in natural condition.

**Why it matters:** Serious buyers use "German / ADRK-aligned" as a quality shorthand; structure is verifiable against the standard.

**Voice bounds:** Describe type accurately; no "giant," "oversize," or exaggerated tropes.

**Proof required:** Standard-aligned photography when available; named breeding stock; no invented titles or show results.

**Kind:** Temperament/structure facts from ADRK Standard = **Fact**; Blacksage conformance = **operator-dependent**.

---

### Pillar 2 — Temperament within ADRK bounds

**Message:** Good-natured, placid baseline, devoted, obedient, biddable, eager to work; self-assured, steady, fearless, **even-tempered** — bred for steadiness, not spectacle.

**Why it matters:** Temperament is breed-defining (companion, service, working dog); aggression marketing is an eliminating fault context and attracts wrong buyers.

**Voice bounds:** Never "guard dog," "protection machine," "weapon," or machismo tropes. Power ≠ aggression. Acknowledge responsible ownership.

**Proof required:** Temperament screening process (BH/ZTP-equivalent concepts); calm imagery direction; operator-described evaluation practices.

**Kind:** ADRK temperament descriptors = **Fact**; Blacksage process claims = **operator-dependent**.

---

### Pillar 3 — Verifiable health transparency

**Message:** Health clearances inform every pairing — hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac, JLPP, and related tests as applicable. Claims are verifiable, not vague.

**Why it matters:** Trust signal #1 in category; buyers OFA-lookup parents before contact.

**Voice bounds:** Never "100% healthy," "disease-free," or guaranteed outcomes. Name **categories** when operator confirms; link to registries when data exists.

**Proof required:** Named tests per dog; OFA/CHIC-equivalent links; health education content.

**Kind:** OFA/CHIC requirements = **Fact**; Blacksage test inventory = **operator-dependent (blocking)**.

---

### Pillar 4 — Deliberate placement, not volume

**Message:** Placements are selective. Blacksage is not a volume kennel. Inquiry and application are mutual fit checks — not checkout.

**Why it matters:** Trust signal #3 (buyer screening); distinguishes ethical tier from puppy-mill UX; matches 6–12+ month waitlist norm.

**Voice bounds:** No fake scarcity, countdown timers, or "only X puppies left." Selectivity framed as care, not FOMO.

**Proof required:** Application/waitlist process description; response expectations; contract/guarantee availability when operator provides.

**Kind:** Category waitlist norm = **Fact**; Blacksage process = **operator-dependent (Q1, Q7)**.

---

### Pillar 5 — Education before sale

**Message:** Buyers who understand the breed standard, health tests, and responsible ownership make better matches. We teach before we ask.

**Why it matters:** Trust signal #5; top competitors publish health/education hubs; reduces unqualified inquiries.

**Voice bounds:** Authoritative but accessible; reward standards literacy; no condescension toward sincere researchers.

**Proof required:** Educational content on standard, health tests (ZTP/BH concepts), puppy-raising process, finding-a-breeder alignment.

**Kind:** Education as category pattern = **Fact**; Blacksage content depth = **Inference** (strategy direction).

## Primary conversion

### Conversion action

**Qualified inquiry / interest or waitlist registration** — initiated **after** trust content, not as site entry point.

### Conversion narrative (buyer journey alignment)

```
DISCOVER → SHORTLIST → VERIFY (on-site) → CONTACT/APPLY
                              ↑
                    Blacksage web job ends here well
```

| Stage | Site responsibility | CTA posture |
|-------|----------------------|-------------|
| Shortlist | Evidence density in first 3–5 screens: who we are, dogs/health/education signals | **No primary apply CTA** |
| Verify | Named stock, health categories, standard alignment, process transparency | Education + proof |
| Contact | Application or interest form; phone if operator provides | **"Begin your inquiry"** — not "Buy," "Reserve," "Apply now" |

### CTA language locks

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| Begin your inquiry | Apply now / Get your puppy |
| Submit inquiry | Buy / Shop / Reserve |
| Inquiry received | You're in! / Application approved |
| Interest list / waitlist (body copy, when Q1 clarified) | Limited time / Only X left |

**Kind:** **Decision** (D2) + **Inference** (category buyer journey).

### IA template (competitive norm — for downstream phases)

Home → About (program/operator) → Males / Females (or Dogs) → Litters / Puppies → Health & Education → Contact / Apply

**Note:** Scroll-driven 3D hero is **de-prioritized**; if any experiential layer is added later, it must not precede or replace proof sections (Phase 3 go/no-go on 3D remains open for CEO).

## Voice and tone

### Brand personality

Confident · Calm · Precise · Evidence-led · Respectful

### Tone bounds

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Speak in ADRK-informed temperament language (devoted, biddable, even-tempered) | Guard-dog aggression stereotypes or machismo |
| Reward buyer research and standards literacy | Condescend to sincere inquirers |
| Acknowledge breed power and owner responsibility | Promise "safe with everyone always" or "non-aggressive" absolutes |
| Frame selectivity as mutual fit | Elitism, shame, or "you can't handle this" gatekeeping |
| Use prose that describes the program clearly | Keyword stuffing ("German Rottweiler 8k supreme") |
| Be honest about unknowns (coming-soon posture when Q1 requires) | Invent litters, locations, or test results |

### Tagline direction (optional — not locked)

Prior stub used **"Power with nobility."** Phase 3 direction favors **evidence-led credibility** over poetic taglines until operator brand preference is confirmed. If used, it must not substitute for proof sections.

**Kind:** Voice bounds = **Decision**; tagline = **Assumption** (operator preference pending).

### Words to use / avoid

| Prefer | Avoid |
|--------|-------|
| Structure, temperament, devoted, deliberate, inquiry, placement, standard-aligned | Guard dog, attack, weapon, fur baby |
| Health clearances, verifiable, OFA/CHIC (when accurate) | 100% healthy, disease-free, rare/exotic |
| ADRK-aligned, FCI Standard No. 147, natural tail | Giant, oversized, supreme, #1 |
| Even-tempered, biddable, self-assured | Killer, protection machine, vicious |

## Competitive framing

### How premium kennels win (8-kennel scan)

1. Named breeding stock on every litter  
2. Specific health credentials (OFA, ADRK HD/ED, JLPP)  
3. Club affiliations visible  
4. Working + conformation proof where applicable  
5. Education before sale  
6. Waitlist / deposit discipline  
7. Published contracts  
8. Real photography  
9. Operator identity  
10. Standard IA (see conversion section)
