## Before you shoot

### Do

- Shoot in **natural or soft controlled light** — true-to-life color for light UI (paper `#FAFAF8`, tan `#8B7355`, sage `#5C6B5E`)
- Show **structure, markings, calm alert expression** — devoted, not aggressive
- Use **consistent aspect ratios** per template below
- Label files clearly: `blacksage_{subject}_{role}_{YYYYMMDD}_{seq}.jpg`
- Deliver **WebP/AVIF-ready masters** at 2× display size; target ≤200KB optimized for hero

### Don't

- Snarling, bared-teeth, spiked-collar, or guard-dog machismo poses
- Stock or AI imagery passed off as Blacksage dogs
- Cinematic crushed blacks that hide tan marking definition
- Full-bleed dark cinematic frames — photos sit in **white or paper-warm cards** on site
- Invent names, health results, litter dates, or location in filenames or captions until operator confirms Tier 2 facts

### Alt text rule

Accurate description only: name (when approved), role (sire/dam), what's visible. No marketing fluff.

## B-roll library (optional brand film & social-later)

Shoot only if time allows in same session — **not required for v1 web launch**.

| Clip ID | Duration | Subject | Camera | Audio |
|---------|----------|---------|--------|-------|
| BR01 | 5–8s | Empty kennel yard, morning mist | Slow static or gentle pan | Ambient only |
| BR02 | 5–8s | Sage stem in breeze, shallow DOF | Static | Ambient |
| BR03 | 5–8s | Hands on leash clip / gate latch (no face required) | Close, static | Ambient |
| BR04 | 8–12s | Approved dog slow walk parallel to camera | Tracking at low angle | Ambient; no music in-camera |
| BR05 | 3–5s | Tan marking detail rack focus | Macro, static | Ambient |

**Framing rules for B-roll:** No snarling, no low-angle "power" shots, no whip pans, no FOMO pacing.
