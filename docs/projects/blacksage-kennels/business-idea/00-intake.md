## One-sentence idea

A German / ADRK-aligned Rottweiler kennel (Blacksage Kennels) that needs coherent business packaging and a public web presence so serious buyers can evaluate the program and apply — without inventing pedigree theater or jumping to design before strategy.

## Trigger

**Lived problem / quality failure** — Operator wanted a kennel website and authorized a light fast-forward to brand/web/build. The resulting site did not meet quality expectations. Root issue for this restart: **solutioning before framing** (no validated customer, geography, program maturity, success criteria, or site job). Rebuild and redesign are out of scope until intake and upstream phases are done.

## 12-month success (assumption — confirm with operator)

- Kennel can be described clearly to a serious buyer: positioning, standards alignment (ADRK/FCI-informed), and how to inquire/apply  
- A **production-quality** web presence (or equivalent channel plan) that operator accepts as on-brand — not the rejected v1 prototype  
- Defined process for applications / waitlist / inquiry handling (destination TBD)  
- Clear go/no-go on whether the website is the primary growth lever vs. a trust/credibility channel for an already-running breeding program  

*Assumptions:* “success” includes a shippable site within 12 months; photography and real contact details become available before launch; no invented litter calendar.
