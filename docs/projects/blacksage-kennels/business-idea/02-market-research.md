## Executive summary

**Serious Rottweiler buyers** — people seeking a 10+ year companion with working aptitude, not an impulse purchase — treat breeder selection as **due diligence on health, temperament, structure, and program integrity**. They research breed standards, health testing (hips, elbows, JLPP, cardiac, eyes), pedigree depth, and breeder screening practices **before** initiating contact. Reputable programs typically run **waitlists of 6–12+ months**, require applications and interviews, and price pet-quality puppies in a **~$1,500–$2,500** band at the mainstream ethical tier, with **$3,000–$7,000+** reported for premium/import/show-adjacent lines ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/pet-insurance/knowledge/how-much-is-a-rottweiler/); [King Rottweilers](https://www.kingrottweilers.com/rottweiler-puppy-cost/); [Lancaster Puppies market copy](https://www.lancasterpuppies.com/sale/puppies/rottweiler/)).

**ADRK/FCI alignment** matters to this segment as a **quality shorthand**: the ADRK standard defines correct structure, natural tail, and temperament (good-natured, devoted, eager to work, self-assured, even-tempered) ([ADRK Standard](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/standard)). ADRK breeding rules require pedigree traceability, radiologic hip/elbow clearance, companion dog (BH) and breed suitability (ZTP) testing before breeding ([ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen)). US buyers evaluating “German-aligned” programs cross-check **OFA/CHIC** (hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac; JLPP strongly recommended) ([OFA CHIC — Rottweiler](https://ofa.org/chic-programs/browse-by-breed/?breed=RO); [Embark — Rottweiler](https://embarkvet.com/resources/https-embarkvet-com-resources-genetic-health-testing-for-rottweilers/)).

**Trust online is the conversion gate.** AKC, ARC, and practitioner literature converge: buyers expect **verifiable health results** (not claims), **transparent pedigrees**, **real photos/video of dogs and facility**, **breeder interview of the buyer**, and **clear contracts/waitlist process** ([AKC — Responsible Breeder](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/dog-breeding/signs-of-a-responsible-breeder/); [AMRRC — Choosing a Breeder](https://www.amrottclub.org/about-the-rottweiler/find-a-rottweiler/choose-breeder/); [The Canine Chasm — Waitlist](https://thecaninechasm.com/how-to-get-on-the-list-reputable-breeders-part-4/)). v1 Blacksage failed holistically on visual polish, 3D, trust/content, and UX/conversion (Phase 1 fact) — which maps directly to what this buyer segment needs **before** they will apply or refer.

## ADRK / FCI-informed expectations

### Breed purpose and temperament (FCI Standard No. 147)

| Domain | ADRK/FCI expectation | Implication for messaging bounds |
|--------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Utilization | Companion, service, and working dog | Working aptitude is **breed-defining**, not a marketing add-on ([ADRK Standard](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/standard)) |
| Temperament | Good-natured, placid baseline, devoted, obedient, biddable, eager to work; self-assured, steady, fearless, even-tempered | Copy must not promise “guard dog aggression” or “attack” positioning ([ADRK Standard](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/standard)) |
| Structure | Medium-large, compact, powerful; black with rich tan markings; correct proportions | Visual content should reflect **standard type**, not exaggerated “giant” tropes |
| Tail | **Natural tail** in natural condition — docking non-conforming under FCI/ADRK ([ADRK Standard](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/standard); [4 Rottweilers](https://www.4rottweilers.com/rottweiler_natural_tail.htm)) |
| Size | Males ~61–68 cm / ~50 kg; females ~56–63 cm / ~42 kg | Size claims should align with standard, not oversize hype |

### ADRK breeding system (Germany)

Per ADRK, **only** Rottweilers with ADRK derivation, pedigree, **radiologic hip and elbow tests**, passed **companion dog (BH)** and **breed suitability (ZTP)** may be bred within the club ([ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen)).

**ZTP (Zuchttauglichkeitsprüfung)** — breed suitability test ([King Rottweilers summary of ADRK rules](https://www.kingrottweilers.com/breed-suitability-testing/)):

- Minimum age 18 months; requires BH certificate  
- Requires hip/elbow certificates recognized by ADRK  
- Two-part assessment: temperament typicality, then performance/confidence (e.g. VPG trials)  
- Disqualifiers include noise sensitivity, incorrect eye color, missing incisors/canines  

**Tail / docking context:** Germany banned cosmetic tail docking (1999); ADRK revised standard to natural tail; FCI member countries phased in compliance ([4 Rottweilers](https://www.4rottweilers.com/rottweiler_natural_tail.htm)). US AKC historically differed; AKC has moved toward natural tail language in standard updates (community discussion: [Reddit — AKC natural tails](https://www.reddit.com/r/Rottweiler/comments/1dkx39e/akc_rottie_standard_now_includes_natural_tails/)). **Serious ADRK-aligned buyers** may view docked tails as a **signal mismatch** with European correctness.

### US parallel — OFA/CHIC (Rottweiler)

| Test | CHIC status | Notes |
|------|-------------|-------|
| Hip dysplasia (OFA radiograph) | **Required** | OFA Rottweiler hip normal rate ~76.9% ([OFA CHIC](https://ofa.org/chic-programs/browse-by-breed/?breed=RO)) |
| Elbow dysplasia | **Required** | Normal ~60.8% in OFA population |
| ACVO eye exam | **Required** | Once ≥24 months |
| Cardiac evaluation | **Required** | Min age 24 months (ARC rule per OFA) |
| JLPP (DNA) | **Optional** but ARC requires one parent clear before breeding | ~8% carrier rate in Embark Rottweiler sample ([Embark](https://embarkvet.com/resources/https-embarkvet-com-resources-genetic-health-testing-for-rottweilers/)) |
| Microchip/tattoo | **Required for CHIC #** | Permanent ID ([OFA CHIC](https://ofa.org/chic-programs/browse-by-breed/?breed=RO)) |

**Inference:** Blacksage positioning as “ADRK-aligned” in the US market will be evaluated against **both** FCI type/temperament norms **and** OFA-verifiable health transparency — buyers cross-reference databases ([King Rottweilers — OFA lookup](https://www.kingrottweilers.com/rottweiler-puppy-cost/)).

## Purchase journey map

```
DISCOVER → SHORTLIST → VERIFY → CONTACT → APPLY → INTERVIEW → DEPOSIT → WAIT → MATCH → PICKUP
```

| Stage | Buyer actions | Friction / drop-off | Channel |
|-------|---------------|---------------------|---------|
| **1. Discover** | Google, breed clubs (ARC referral), social, dog shows, word of mouth | Overwhelmed by ads, puppy mills, “German Rottweiler” SEO spam | Search, Facebook/Instagram, club directories ([AMRRC](https://www.amrottclub.org/about-the-rottweiler/find-a-rottweiler/choose-breeder/)) |
| **2. Shortlist** | Open 3–8 breeder sites; scan health, dogs, tone | Sites with no dogs named, no tests, generic copy → **eliminated** | Website (first impression) |
| **3. Verify** | OFA database lookup; read pedigree; compare to standard | Claims without links; inconsistent info | OFA.org, Embark, ADRK resources |
| **4. Contact** | Thoughtful email: lifestyle, breed goals, why this breeder | “How much? puppies available?” → ignored/blacklisted | Email, phone per breeder rules ([The Canine Chasm](https://thecaninechasm.com/how-to-get-on-the-list-reputable-breeders-part-4/)) |
| **5. Apply** | Complete questionnaire honestly | Refusing application → no conversation | PDF/web form |
| **6. Interview** | Phone/video/in-person; discuss training, vet, rehome policy | Misaligned expectations on energy, cropping, guard work | Phone, visit |
| **7. Deposit** | Refundable/non-refundable per contract; timing varies (pre-birth to 2–4 weeks old) | Scam fear if trust not established | PayPal, check, contract |
| **8. Wait** | 6–12+ months common; follow social for litter news | Impatience → leaves for BYB | Email, social |
| **9. Match** | Breeder assigns puppy — buyer often **does not pick** | Demanding pick-of-litter → off list | Breeder-led |
| **10. Pickup / ship** | Visit strongly preferred; health cert, microchip, papers | Shipping-only without relationship = red flag for many | In-person ideal ([AKC](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/dog-breeding/signs-of-a-responsible-breeder/)) |

**Typical timeline:** Plan **months to years** from first contact to puppy home ([The Canine Chasm](https://thecaninechasm.com/how-to-get-on-the-list-reputable-breeders-part-4/) — author report of 3-year wait for one puppy).

**Friction points most relevant to web presence:**

1. **Shortlist elimination** — no verifiable proof on site  
2. **Contact abandonment** — apply UX broken, unclear instructions, or “salesy” tone  
3. **Deposit hesitation** — insufficient legitimacy signals  
4. **Waitlist dropout** — no ongoing communication touchpoints (optional newsletter/social)
