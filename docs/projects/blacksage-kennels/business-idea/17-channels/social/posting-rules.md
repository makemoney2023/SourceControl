# Social Posting Rules — Blacksage Kennels

**Phase:** 17  
**Last updated:** 2026-07-27  
**Applies to:** Facebook (optional) · Instagram (optional, Q6-gated)

---

## Voice & tone

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Confident, calm, precise | Hype, emoji-heavy, exclamation-led |
| Evidence-led | Vague superlatives ("best," "#1," "perfect family dog") |
| Respectful | Gatekeeping, shame-based, macho |
| Educational | Sales-forward, urgency-driven |

**Reading level:** Grade 8–10. Pair breed-standard terms with plain-English gloss.

---

## Do

- Link every post to a **specific owned URL** (`/health#section`, `/dogs`, `/about`, `/inquire`)
- Use **Begin your inquiry** (Tier 2 / Package B) or **Join our interest list** (Tier 1 / Package A) as the only conversion labels
- Lead with **education** — one idea per post
- Use brand mark or **Q6-verified operator photography** only
- Reply to comments with calm facts + site link
- Mark program stage honestly ("profiles coming soon," "program in development")
- Pause posting if site education (E1–E8) is stubbed — social amplifies owned content, not substitutes
- Coordinate with lifecycle-marketer — avoid same theme on email within 48 hours

---

## Don't

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| Buy · Shop · Reserve · Apply now · Get your puppy | CTA locks |
| Price, deposit amounts, "DM for price" | A10 · off-site only after qualification |
| "Only X left," "Going fast," countdowns, "Available now" | Anti-FOMO / selectivity pillar |
| Guard dog, protection dog, weapon, attack language | Brand guardrail |
| Stock dog photos, AI dogs as program dogs | Tier 3 · SD5 |
| Boosted posts / paid puppy ads | Phase 19 skipped |
| Weekly litter updates or availability spam | Education > litter drops |
| Named OFA results, litter dates without operator sign-off | Tier 2 gate |
| `[LOCATION]` or `[CONTACT_EMAIL]` until Q2 verified | Placeholder harm |
| "ADRK certified" unless affiliation verified | Claims guardrail |
| Puppy-face as profile logo | Brand mark from `11-brand-system.md` |
| Engagement bait ("Tag a friend who needs a Rottweiler!") | Low-trust pattern |

## CTA rules

### Hierarchy (match site)

| Priority | Label | When | Link |
|----------|-------|------|------|
| 1 | Read more / View our health approach | Most posts | `/health` or anchor |
| 2 | View our dogs | Dog/program status posts | `/dogs` |
| 3 | Learn our process | Placement posts | `/health#placement` |
| 4 | **Join our interest list** | Tier 1; max 1/month | `/inquire` |
| 4 | **Begin your inquiry** | Tier 2; max 1/month | `/inquire` |

**Rule:** At least **3 education posts** between any two inquire CTAs.

### Bio / link-in-bio

- **Tier 1 default:** Site home or `/health`
- **Tier 2 option:** `/inquire` acceptable in bio after E1–E8 live
- **Never:** Payment links, Linktree of off-brand funnels, marketplace listings

---

## Q6 media gate

| Q6 status | Facebook | Instagram |
|-----------|----------|-----------|
| **No verified dog media** | Text + link posts; brand graphics OK | **Skip Instagram** OR text-on-brand carousel only — no stock dogs |
| **Operator photography verified** | Single-photo education posts OK | Carousels + single photo OK |
| **Partial (brand only)** | Brand/environment cover + mark | Brand aesthetic posts only |

**Never post:** Stock photography presented as Blacksage dogs · aggression imagery (spiked collars, bared teeth) · puppy-mill aesthetic

**Alt text (when images used):** Descriptive, factual — "[Dog name], [role], [setting]" when Tier 2; "Blacksage Kennels brand mark" for graphics.

---

## Empty-state / Tier 1 rules

When Q1 = brand-first (Package A only):

- Dogs pillar posts use **honest empty-state language** — link to `/health`, not fake profiles
- No implied availability ("coming soon puppies," "next litter")
- Interest list language only — "not a reservation"
- About posts may reference `[OPERATOR_STORY]` gap honestly when operator story pending

---

## Litter & availability (rare / verified-only)

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| **Default** | Do not schedule litter posts in calendar |
| **Exception trigger** | Operator verifies litter facts + site `/dogs` or future `/litters` page updated **first** |
| **Max frequency** | 1 announcement per verified litter — not a series |
| **Required language** | Factual only; link to site; **Begin your inquiry** — never "reserve" |
| **Prohibited** | "X puppies left," sex breakdown as urgency, price hints, home-by dates as FOMO |

---

## Comment & DM policy

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| "How much?" | "We discuss investment after mutual fit — not on social. Our site explains the inquiry process: [link to `/health#placement`]" |
| "Any puppies available?" | "We place selectively — explore our program on site and begin an inquiry when ready: [link]" |
| Guard-dog request | Calm disqualification + temperament education link |
| Education question | Answer briefly + link to relevant `/health` anchor |
| Spam / trolls | Hide or delete; do not argue publicly |

**Do not:** Quote price in comments · Confirm waitlist placement · Promise timelines in DMs

---

## Pre-publish checklist

- [ ] Post maps to one pillar in `content-pillars.md`
- [ ] Link points to live owned URL (not placeholder domain)
- [ ] CTA label matches Package A or B tier
- [ ] No Tier 3 claims (price, fake dogs, FOMO, guard-dog)
- [ ] Media passes Q6 gate
- [ ] Operator approved (recommended for Tier 2 dog/litter content)
- [ ] Not duplicating email theme this week

---

## Pause / stop conditions

Stop posting immediately if:

- Site returns Tier 3 claims or stub education
- Operator requests hold
- Inbound DMs skew anti-persona (price-only, guard-dog) — fix messaging, don't increase frequency
- Q7 inquiry routing broken

**Silence > off-brand posting.** Owned + borrowed channels carry GTM without rented social.
