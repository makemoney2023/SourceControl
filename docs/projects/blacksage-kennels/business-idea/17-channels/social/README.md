## Purpose

This folder defines **optional rented social** (Facebook and/or Instagram) as a **support channel** for credibility and education — not proof, not acquisition volume, and not litter marketing.

**North star:** Every post earns trust or teaches something useful; every link returns buyers to the **owned website** for verification and inquiry.

**GTM posture (Phase 6):** Owned + borrowed is enough at launch. Social is **P3 — optional 2–4 posts/month**. Skip entirely if operator lacks capacity.

## Cadence recommendation

| Operator capacity | Recommendation |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **No dedicated time** | **Skip rented social** at launch. Owned site + borrowed referrals carry GTM. |
| **Minimal (1–2 hrs/month)** | **One platform** (Facebook preferred if any) · **2 posts/month** · education-only |
| **Moderate (2–4 hrs/month)** | One platform · **3–4 posts/month** · mix education + program/process |
| **Full-time marketing team** | Not assumed in v1 bootstrapped GTM — revisit after Tier 2 proof exists |

**Default for Phase 17 planning:** 3 posts/month on a single platform, or **skip**.

---

## Capacity gate (operator decision)

Before creating or claiming any rented profile:

- [ ] Operator confirms **2–4 hours/month** for drafting, scheduling, and replying to comments (not DMs for price)
- [ ] **Q7** inquiry destination live — social bios link to owned site `/inquire`, not DM commerce
- [ ] **E1–E8** education substantive on site — social amplifies owned content, not substitutes for it
- [ ] **Q6** status known — if no verified dog media, commit to education-only posts (see `posting-rules.md`)
- [ ] Operator accepts **no paid boost** in base case (Phase 19 deferred)

If any gate fails → **skip rented social** until resolved. This is the recommended default for Tier 1 brand-first launch.

## What social is not

| Not social's job | Where that lives |
|------------------|------------------|
| Primary proof surface | Owned website (`/health`, `/dogs`, `/about`) |
| Inquiry capture / CRM | Owned `/inquire` + email (lifecycle-marketer) |
| Litter announcements / availability | Rare, verified-only; site `/dogs` or future `/litters` is canonical |
| Price or deposit discussion | Off-site after qualification (sales playbook) |
| Paid acquisition | Phase 19 — **skipped** unless operator funds |

---

## CTA lock (all social)

**Primary link:** Canonical site URL → `/health` or `/inquire` as context dictates  
**Primary label in copy:** **Begin your inquiry** (Package B) or **Join our interest list** (Package A / Tier 1)  
**Never:** Buy · Shop · Reserve · Apply now · DM for price · Available now
