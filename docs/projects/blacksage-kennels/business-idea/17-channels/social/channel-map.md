## Owned channels (primary — not operated in this folder)

| Asset | Role in social strategy | Social integration |
|-------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| **Website** (`/`, `/health`, `/dogs`, `/about`) | Canonical proof + education | Every social post links here — deep link to relevant section |
| **`/inquire`** | Conversion after trust | Bio link + occasional tertiary CTA in post copy |
| **Interest / inquiry email** | Package A nurture | Do not duplicate weekly email content on social same day |
| **Search (SEO)** | Discovery | Social supports `/health` pillar indexing via shares — not a ranking substitute |

**Bio / link-in-bio URL:** Production site root or `/health` — **never** a Linktree of off-brand funnels.

---

## Borrowed channels (supporting — relationship-led)

Social does **not** replace borrowed discovery. Referrers remain highest-quality path.

| Channel | Role | Social interaction |
|---------|------|-------------------|
| Trainer referrals | Highest-quality leads | Trainers share **site URL**, not social profile — social is optional amplifier |
| Breed club / sport networks | Credibility adjacency | Contribute value in communities first; **no kennel ads in forums** |
| Prior owner WOM | Post-placement proof | Testimonials on site when verified — social may reshare **only with written consent** |
| Show / working contacts | Sub-segment discovery | Tier 2 only; link to `/dogs` when profiles verified |

**Social's job for borrowed:** Give referrers a **respectable public face** they can mention — evidence-dense site remains the share target.

## Rented channels (optional, capped)

### Platform matrix

| Platform | Priority | Best for | Reference images | Status |
|----------|----------|----------|------------------|--------|
| **Facebook Page** | **Preferred first** (if any) | Longer education posts, link posts to `/health`, 35+ demographic research-heavy buyers | Up to 4 (if using FLUX metaphor — N/A here) | Optional |
| **Instagram** | Second (or skip) | Real operator media when **Q6 satisfied**; brand aesthetic | Requires verified photography | Optional — Q6 gated |
| **TikTok** | — | — | — | **Never** — wrong tone/category for D2 |
| **X / Twitter** | — | — | — | **Skip** — low ICP fit, high noise |
| **YouTube** | — | — | — | **Defer** — capacity |
| **Pinterest** | — | — | — | **Skip** v1 |

### Facebook (preferred first rented platform)

**Why first (GTM):** Primary ICP (serious, research-heavy buyers 35+) often discovers via search and Facebook breed groups; link posts to owned education travel well; no dependency on daily Stories cadence.

| Use | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Profile type** | Business Page (not personal profile for kennel commerce) |
| **Bio** | One line positioning + link to site `/health` or home |
| **Post formats** | Text + link (priority), single photo (Q6 only), album (rare) |
| **Frequency** | 2–4 posts/month support-only |
| **CTA pattern** | "Read more on our site →" linking to `/health#testing` etc. |
| **Comments** | Reply with education + link to site; **never quote price in comments** |
| **Messenger** | Do not enable "DM for puppy" workflow; redirect to `/inquire` |

### Instagram (optional second — or skip)

**Gate:** **Q6 photography** — real operator media required. Without Q6, use **text-on-brand graphic** posts only or **skip Instagram entirely**.

| Use | Detail |
|-----|--------|
| **Bio link** | Site URL — `/health` or `/inquire` per tier |
| **Post formats** | Carousel education (standards, health categories), single photo (verified dogs only) |
| **Stories** | Optional; same education snippets — no countdown stickers, no "available" polls |
| **Reels** | Defer — capacity + trend risk |
| **Frequency** | Same 2–4/month total across platforms if both active — not doubled |

## Start-with-one recommendation

```
IF operator has ≤4 hrs/month for social:
  IF operator already uses Facebook personally for breed content:
    → Start Facebook Page only
  ELSE IF operator has Q6 photography + visual comfort:
    → Consider Instagram only
  ELSE:
    → SKIP rented social — owned + borrowed only

IF operator later adds capacity:
  → Add second platform only after 90 days consistent cadence on first
```

**Default recommendation for Tier 1 launch:** **Skip rented social** OR **Facebook only, 2 posts/month, education-only** until E1–E8 live and M5 referrer sniff test passes.

---

## Skip conditions (do not launch rented social)

| Condition | Why skip |
|-----------|----------|
| Operator cannot commit 2+ hrs/month | Shelfware damages credibility more than silence |
| E1–E8 education stubs on site | Social would drive traffic to empty proof |
| Q7 inquiry destination not live | Bio links dead-end |
| No Q6 media and unwilling to use text/brand graphics only | Instagram especially — stock dogs prohibited |
| Operator wants litter/availability marketing | Violates D2 — social plan incompatible |
| Expectation of lead volume from social | Wrong metric; GTM is quality > volume |

## What never to post (any platform)

| Category | Examples |
|----------|----------|
| **Price / commerce** | "$X,XXX," deposits, "DM for price," payment links |
| **FOMO / scarcity** | "Only 2 left," "Going fast," countdown timers, "Reserve now" |
| **Availability spam** | "Puppies available now," weekly litter updates |
| **Guard-dog tropes** | Protection marketing, aggression, weapons language |
| **Stock / fake proof** | Stock dog photos, AI dogs presented as program dogs |
| **Unverified claims** | "ADRK certified," named OFA results, litter dates without operator sign-off |
| **Boosted puppy ads** | Any paid promotion of availability |
| **Marketplace cross-posts** | PuppyFind-class listings |
| **Apply-first urgency** | "Apply now," "Limited spots on waitlist" |

## Measurement (quality > volume)

| Track | Ignore |
|-------|--------|
| Clicks to `/health` and `/inquire` from social | Raw follower count |
| Inquiries tagging "Social media" in "How did you hear about us?" | Viral reach |
| Referrer willingness to share URL (M5) | Engagement rate as primary KPI |
| Comment quality (education questions vs. price DMs) | Post frequency streaks |

**Not success:** Follower growth campaigns · viral puppy reels · price inquiry volume in DMs.
