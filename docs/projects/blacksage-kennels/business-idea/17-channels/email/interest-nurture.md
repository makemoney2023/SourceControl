# Interest List Nurture (Package A)

**Journey:** Education-led nurture for interest list members  
**Trigger:** Confirmed Package A membership (post triage)  
**Goal:** Build trust through standards, health, temperament, and placement literacy — without FOMO or price  
**Cadence:** ~3–6 weeks between sends; pause if no verified program news  
**Exit:** Buyer submits Package B inquiry when waitlist opens; unsubscribes; or goes dormant (operator-defined, e.g., 90 days no engagement)

---

| # | Name | Send | Primary topic |

**Send:** 6–8 weeks after Email 1 (or ~9–12 weeks from join)  
**Segment:** Package A — active members

**Subject:** What "ADRK-aligned" means in practice

**Preview:** FCI Standard No. 147 — type, markings, and natural tail — without trend breeding language.

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

Buyers often ask what separates a deliberate German-type Rottweiler program from volume production. A useful starting point is the breed standard itself.

The Rottweiler is defined by **FCI Standard No. 147** — the reference for ADRK-aligned breeding. Correct type includes:

- Black coat with clearly defined rich tan markings  
- Compact, powerful build — not oversized or "XL" trend framing  
- Natural tail in natural condition (German / ADRK context)  

At Blacksage, breeding decisions reference this standard. Structure and temperament inform pairings — not fashion or hype.

**Send:** 10–12 weeks after Email 1 (or ~15–20 weeks from join)  
**Segment:** Package A — active members

**Subject:** How responsible Rottweiler programs approach health testing

**Preview:** Hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac, JLPP — what categories mean and why verification matters.

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

Health claims are easy to make and harder to verify. We prefer the second path.

Responsible Rottweiler breeding programs evaluate breeding stock across categories that matter for the breed — including hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac screening, and JLPP testing when applicable. Results, when published, should link to independent registries such as OFA and CHIC so you can verify without taking our word for it.

Our site describes **what we test and why** — at the category level today, with per-dog registry links when operator-verified inventory is live:

→ `https://[DOMAIN]/health#testing`

We do not claim "100% healthy" or "disease-free." We describe our approach and point you to evidence when it exists.

**Subject:** Steady, devoted, biddable — not guard-dog marketing

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

Rottweilers are powerful dogs. Responsible programs breed toward **temperament within the standard** — not toward intimidation or guard-dog fantasy.

The FCI standard describes a Rottweiler as good-natured, placid in basic disposition, devoted to its owner, obedient, eager to work, and self-assured — fearless and even-tempered, not nervous or aggressive.

At Blacksage:

- We place toward companion, family, and sport/working homes within ADRK bounds  
- We do **not** market or place for guard work, protection roles, or aggression-forward use cases  
- Power requires responsible ownership — training, management, and fit matter  

If your primary goal is a "protection dog" or status symbol, we may not be the right program. If you want a devoted, even-tempered partner with working capability, read on:

## Cadence notes

- **Do not compress** this sequence into weekly sends — GTM lock is low frequency, high value.  
- **Pause** the sequence if no new verified content exists; resume when operator publishes breeding stock or Tier 2 milestone.  
- **Do not include** pricing, deposit amounts, litter dates, or "spots filling up" language in any email.
