# Tier 2 Program Update (Interest List → Waitlist Open)

**Journey:** One-time announcement when operator promotes Tier 2 / opens waitlist consideration to interest list members  
**Trigger:** Q1 = active program; operator sign-off for Tier 2 promotion; verified Dogs/Health content live  
**Goal:** Inform Package A members that waitlist consideration is available — without FOMO, price, or false urgency  
**Cadence:** 1–2 emails total; not a recurring drip

---

## Sequence overview

| # | Name | Send | Audience |
|---|------|------|----------|
| 1 | Program milestone — waitlist consideration open | Once, on Tier 2 promotion | Package A — active interest list |
| 2 | Reminder for engaged non-responders | 4–6 weeks after Email 1, optional | A members who opened Email 1 but did not inquire for B |

**Gate:** Send only when Tier 2 launch checklist items are met (GTM §3): populated Dogs when verified, zero Tier 3 claims, Q7 live.

---

## Email 1: Waitlist consideration now open

**Send:** Once, on operator Tier 2 promotion date  
**Segment:** All active Package A interest list members  
**Automated:** Operator-triggered broadcast — not recurring

**Subject:** Blacksage update — waitlist consideration is open

**Preview:** Our active program is live on the website. Interest list membership is unchanged — not automatic waitlist placement.

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

You joined our interest list while our program was in development. We committed to share updates when we had news worth sending — not weekly promotions.

**Today’s update:** Blacksage Kennels has published our active program on the website, including breeding stock and health information where verified. We are now accepting inquiries for **waitlist consideration** from buyers who have reviewed our program and believe there may be mutual fit.

**What this means for you**

- **Interest list membership is unchanged.** You are not automatically moved to a waitlist.  
- **Waitlist consideration** is a separate step: a full inquiry with household and experience details, reviewed individually.  
- Submission is **not** approval, reservation, or pricing discussion.  
- We still do not publish prices or collect deposits on the website.  

If you have already done your due diligence on our Health & Education pages and wish to take the next step:

→ **Begin your inquiry** at `https://[DOMAIN]/inquire`

If you prefer to remain on the interest list for occasional updates only, no action is required.

Thank you for your patience while we built the program the way we ask buyers to research — evidence first.

Warm regards,  
[OPERATOR_NAME]  
Blacksage Kennels  
`[CONTACT_EMAIL]`

**CTA:** Begin your inquiry → `https://[DOMAIN]/inquire`

## Email 2: Reminder for engaged interest list members (optional)

**Send:** 4–6 weeks after Email 1, only if operator chooses a single follow-up  
**Segment:** Opened Email 1, did not submit B inquiry, still subscribed  
**Automated:** Optional; many programs skip this email to avoid nagging

**Subject:** Still researching? Our placement process overview

**Preview:** No pressure. One link if waitlist consideration is still on your horizon.

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

A few weeks ago we shared that Blacksage is accepting inquiries for waitlist consideration. We do not send repeated "last chance" reminders — one follow-up is enough.

If you are still evaluating whether our program fits your home and timeline, our placement process page explains how inquiry, waitlist consideration, and placement work — without checkout language or on-site pricing:

→ `https://[DOMAIN]/health#placement`

If the timing is not right, stay on the interest list. We will continue occasional, high-value updates when verified program news exists.

If you are ready:

→ **Begin your inquiry** at `https://[DOMAIN]/inquire`

Warm regards,  
[OPERATOR_NAME]  
Blacksage Kennels  
`[CONTACT_EMAIL]`

**CTA:** Learn our placement process → `https://[DOMAIN]/health#placement`

**Do not send Email 2 if:** No Tier 2 content is live, operator has not confirmed waitlist capacity, or Email 1 was fewer than 4 weeks ago.

## Operator checklist before send

- [ ] Q1 = active program confirmed  
- [ ] Dogs page populated with verified profiles OR honest empty state removed  
- [ ] Health links verified where claimed  
- [ ] `/inquire` Package B mode enabled  
- [ ] Q7 inquiry destination live  
- [ ] Zero Tier 3 claims (prices, fake OFA, invented litters)  
- [ ] No deposit amounts or pricing in email body

| Email 2 send rate | Expect many programs to skip Email 2 entirely |
