Health claims are easy to make and harder to verify. We prefer the second path.
Responsible Rottweiler breeding programs evaluate breeding stock across categories that matter for the breed — including hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac screening, and JLPP testing when applicable. Results, when published, should link to independent registries such as OFA and CHIC so you can verify without taking our word for it.
Our site describes what we test and why — at the category level today, with per-dog registry links when operator-verified inventory is live:
→ https://[DOMAIN]/health#testing
We do not claim "100% healthy" or "disease-free." We describe our approach and point you to evidence when it exists.
