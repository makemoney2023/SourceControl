Rottweilers are powerful dogs. Responsible programs breed toward temperament within the standard — not toward intimidation or guard-dog fantasy.
The FCI standard describes a Rottweiler as good-natured, placid in basic disposition, devoted to its owner, obedient, eager to work, and self-assured — fearless and even-tempered, not nervous or aggressive.
At Blacksage:
  • We place toward companion, family, and sport/working homes within ADRK bounds
  • We do not market or place for guard work, protection roles, or aggression-forward use cases
  • Power requires responsible ownership — training, management, and fit matter
