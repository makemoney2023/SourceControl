# Waitlist Nurture (Package B)

**Journey:** Expectation-setting for waitlist consideration and approved waitlist members  
**Trigger:** Package B form submit and/or operator approval for waitlist consideration  
**Goal:** Clarify mutual fit process, timeline honesty, and next steps — no price in email, no FOMO  
**Cadence:** Event-driven + milestone; not weekly  
**Exit:** Placement (Package C) handoff to CS; release from waitlist; or polite decline

---

## Sequence overview

| # | Name | Send | Audience |
|---|------|------|----------|
| 1 | After review — what we look for | 1–2 weeks post-submit if review ongoing | B submitters awaiting qualification |
| 2 | Waitlist consideration invitation | After B1–B9 pass (operator) | Qualified buyers invited to formal B path |
| 3 | Timeline honesty | After waitlist consideration confirmed | Approved or in-consideration B members |
| 4 | Program update (verified facts only) | When operator has verified litter/stock news | B members in good standing |

**Note:** Email 1 auto-acknowledgment lives in `inquiry-welcome.md` (Email 2). This sequence begins after submit.

## Email 3: Timeline honesty

**Send:** After waitlist consideration is confirmed or buyer is approved for waitlist membership  
**Segment:** Package B — approved or in active waitlist consideration  
**Automated:** No — operator sends when status is clear

**Subject:** Honest timeline expectations — Blacksage waitlist

**Preview:** No invented dates. Category norms vs. our current posture — clearly labeled.

**Body:**

Dear [First Name],

You deserve a clear picture of what waitlist consideration means for timing — without pressure or invented urgency.

**What we can say today**

- Inquiry and interest list membership do **not** hold a place in line  
- Approved waitlist placement, when offered, reflects mutual fit established through our process — not first-come checkout order  
- We plan litters deliberately; we do not operate on an "available now" model  

**Category context (assumption — not a Blacksage guarantee):**  
Responsible Rottweiler programs often involve waits of **six to twelve months or longer**. Individual programs vary with capacity, litter planning, and match quality. We will not quote a specific date unless operator-verified facts support it.

**What we will do**

- Contact you when verified program updates affect your inquiry  
- Reconfirm household and goals before any litter match conversation  
- Provide written terms before any deposit request — off-site, after approval  

**What we will not do**

- Send weekly "puppy availability" promotions  
- Use countdown timers or "another family is interested" pressure  
- Discuss pricing by email blast before you have had a qualification conversation  

If your timeline has changed since your inquiry, reply and let us know. Flexibility works both ways.

Warm regards,  
[OPERATOR_NAME]  
Blacksage Kennels  
`[CONTACT_EMAIL]`
