## Brand essence

**Blacksage Kennels** — German / ADRK-aligned Rottweilers. Prestige through evidence and cinematic presence, not checkout spectacle.

## Color

| Role | Hex |
|------|-----|

| Elevated | `#121212` |
| Lifted | `#1A1A1A` |
| Tan key | `#C4A35A` |
| Tan deep | `#A67C52` |
| Text | `#F3EFE6` / `#A8A49C` |

CTA fill = tan; CTA text = `#070707`. No purple. No cream paper.

## Typography

- **Display:** Fraunces (film titles, H1)
- **UI/Body:** Manrope
- Overlines: Manrope uppercase tracking ~0.2em, tan

## Voice (for copy)

Documentary calm. Short sentences. Standards and health before ask. No aggression marketing.

## Anti-patterns
