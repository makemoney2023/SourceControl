## Executive summary

Evidence supports a **credibility-first kennel + web channel** opportunity for ADRK-aligned Rottweilers — not a cosmetic website rebuild. Serious buyers treat puppy purchase as a **due-diligence decision** (health verification, pedigree review, breeder screening) with **6–12+ month waitlists** and **~$1,500–$2,500** mainstream ethical pricing ([Insurify](https://insurify.com/pet-insurance/knowledge/how-much-is-a-rottweiler/); [The Canine Chasm](https://thecaninechasm.com/how-to-get-on-the-list-reputable-breeders-part-4/)).

**ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147** defines the breed as companion, service, and working dog — good-natured, devoted, biddable, self-assured, even-tempered — with **natural tail** and defined structure ([ADRK Standard](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/standard)). ADRK club breeding requires **HD/ED radiographs, BH companion test, and ZTP breed suitability** ([ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen)). US buyers cross-check **OFA/CHIC** (hips, elbows, eyes, cardiac; JLPP per ARC rule) ([OFA CHIC — Rottweiler](https://ofa.org/chic-programs/browse-by-breed/?breed=RO)).

**Competitive scan (8 premium kennels):** Quality signals are **evidence density** — named parents, specific health certs, club badges, education articles, waitlist/deposit discipline — not cinematic web UX. **Zero competitors** use scroll 3D/WebGL. Several top kennels use dated templates yet win trust through proof ([CI handoff](HANDOFFS/2-competitive-intelligence-analyst.md)).

**Recommendation: Proceed** to Phase 3 strategy with default hypothesis **trust-first (D2)**, not apply-first or 3D-led prestige. **Conditions:** operator must supply program facts, photography, and health inventory before any credible public launch. Do not invent Blacksage location, prices, or litter claims.

### RQ2 — What health and breeding gates does ADRK enforce?

| Requirement | Detail | Source |
|-------------|--------|--------|
| Club breeding only | ADRK derivation, pedigree, HD/ED radiographs, passed BH + ZTP | [ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen) |
| HD grading | HD- through HD+++ with breeding bans at ++ and +++ | [ADRK Health — HD](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/gesundheit) |
| ED grading | ED required since 1996; ED+++ = breeding ban | [ADRK Health — ED](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/rasse/gesundheit) |
| BH | Companion dog test — prerequisite for breeding | [ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen) |
| ZTP | Breed suitability test — temperament + performance gate | [ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen) |
| ADRK scope | Breeding stations and trials limited to Germany; foreign kennels align via ADRK World Family / import pedigrees | [ADRK General Information](https://adrk.de/index.php/en/verein/allgemeine-informationen) |

**US parallel — OFA/CHIC (Rottweiler):**

| Test | CHIC status | Population note |
|------|-------------|-----------------|
| Hips | Required | ~76.9% normal in OFA database ([OFA CHIC](https://ofa.org/chic-programs/browse-by-breed/?breed=RO)) |
| Elbows | Required | ~60.8% normal |
| Eyes (ACVO) | Required | Once ≥24 months |
| Cardiac | Required | Min age 24 months (ARC rule per OFA) |
| JLPP (DNA) | Optional on CHIC; **ARC requires one parent clear** before breeding | ~13.5% carrier rate in OFA JLPP tests |
| Microchip/tattoo | Required for CHIC # | Permanent ID |

**Implication (inference):** US-facing "ADRK-aligned" programs are judged on **verifiable OFA/CHIC-equivalent transparency** even when marketing German lineage.

### RQ3 — How do serious buyers evaluate breeders online?

Synthesized from [02-market-research.md](02-market-research.md), [AKC Responsible Breeder](https://www.akc.org/expert-advice/dog-breeding/signs-of-a-responsible-breeder/), [AMRRC Choose a Breeder](https://www.amrottclub.org/about-the-rottweiler/find-a-rottweiler/choose-breeder/), [The Canine Chasm](https://thecaninechasm.com/how-to-get-on-the-list-reputable-breeders-part-4/).

| Stage | Buyer behavior | Drop-off trigger |
|-------|----------------|------------------|
| Discover | Google, breed clubs, referrals, shows | Puppy-mill SEO noise |
| Shortlist | Scan 3–8 sites for named dogs + tests | No parent info, vague "health tested" |
| Verify | OFA lookup, pedigree review | Claims without registry links |
| Contact | Thoughtful email; breeder interviews buyer | "How much? puppies now?" tone |
| Apply → wait | 6–12+ months; deposit after trust | Apply before proof established |

**Trust signal ranking (evidence-weighted):**

1. Verifiable health clearances (OFA/CHIC, JLPP, cardiac, eyes)  
2. Named breeding stock + pedigrees/titles  
3. Application + buyer screening  
4. Real photography/video (not placeholders)  
5. Educational content on standard and process  
6. Club affiliations (ADRK, USRC, ARC, AKC BOM)  
7. Contracts, guarantees, return policy  
8. Waitlist transparency  
9. Social proof (secondary)  
10. Visual prestige / 3D UX (**low alone**; v1 negative evidence)

### RQ4 — What does "good" look like on premium competitor websites?

Merged from competitive-intelligence IC ([HANDOFFS/2-competitive-intelligence-analyst.md](HANDOFFS/2-competitive-intelligence-analyst.md)). **8 kennels analyzed:** Von Evman, Dreibergen, Old World, Von Ruelmann, Von Der Musikstadt, Vom Haus Burns, Henkershaus (UK), ADRK registry reference.

#### Quality patterns (12)

1. **Named breeding stock** — both parents linked on every litter  
2. **Specific health credentials** — OFA, ADRK HD/ED, JLPP, Embark panels named  
3. **Club affiliations visible** — ADRK, USRC, ARC, AKC Breeder of Merit  
4. **Working + conformation proof** — Schutzhund/IGP, ZTP, Sieger titles  
5. **Education before sale** — breed standard, finding a breeder, health articles  
6. **Waitlist / deposit discipline** — ~$500 deposit common; scarcity normal  
7. **Published contracts** — purchase agreements downloadable  
8. **Real photography** — whelping, kennel, show, socialization  
9. **Operator identity** — named people, years, location, phone  
10. **Standard IA** — Home → About → Males → Females → Puppies/Litters → Health/Education → Contact  
11. **Phone + form hybrid** — phone for serious buyers; form captures interest  
12. **Multi-channel updates** — Facebook/YouTube for litter news; owned site for durable proof  

**Visual bar:** Prestige = **evidence density**, not design novelty. Dated Jimdo/HTML/WordPress templates tolerated when proof is abundant. **No competitor uses scroll 3D/WebGL.**

#### Red flags (12)

1. Vague "health tested" without named tests  
2. Puppies without linked sire/dam  
3. Always-available puppies, no waitlist  
4. Stock/generic imagery  
5. No ADRK/FCI/USRC/ARC/OFA alignment  
6. Payment pressure before contract review  
7. No contract or return policy  
8. Marketplace-only presence (no owned program site)  
9. Site neglect / spam injection  
10. Superlatives without titles/certs  
11. Shopping-cart checkout UX  
12. Geographic/identity opacity  

#### Competitive set snapshot

| Kennel | URL | Standout signal |
|--------|-----|-----------------|
| Von Evman | https://vonevmans.com/ | OFA on all parents; ARC Gold; extensive real-photo gallery |
| Dreibergen | https://www.4rottweilers.com/ | Published purchase agreement; $500 waitlist policy; ADRK/USRC badges |
| Old World | https://www.oldworldkennel.com/ | ADRK partner kennel in Germany; import pedigree path |
| Von Ruelmann | https://www.vr-rottweilers.com/ | Reserve form + per-dog OFA/JLPP pages; 45+ years |
| Von Der Musikstadt | https://www.vdrrottweilerbreeders.com/ | Deep health education hub; downloadable contracts |
| Vom Haus Burns | https://vomhausburns.com/ | Education reframing AKC vs quality; show results log |
| Henkershaus | https://www.rottweiler-breeder.com/henkershaus-rottweilers | UK import/working proof (aggregator-hosted) |

**Pricing transparency:** **0/8** competitors list puppy prices on site — price discussed after qualification.

### RQ5 — How does evidence map to Blacksage v1 failure modes?

Per [01-problem-framing.md](01-problem-framing.md), v1 failed **holistically** (visual, 3D, trust/content, UX/conversion). **Inference only** — no Blacksage program facts asserted.

| v1 layer | Category baseline | Evidence-based gap |
|----------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Visual polish | Dated UI OK if proof-rich; real dog photos carry prestige | Placeholders + 3D without authentic media fail category norm |
| 3D / experiential | **Absent in all 8 premium competitors** | 3D was unvalidated tactic; adds complexity without trust ROI |
| Trust / content | Named dogs, certs, education, contracts, operator story | v1 lacked operator-supplied evidence layer |
| UX / conversion | Waitlist + phone + qualification; not e-commerce checkout | Apply-first without trust prerequisites inverts buyer journey |

**Root cause alignment (Phase 1):** Failure is consistent with **solutioning before strategy** — investing in spectacle (3D) and conversion (apply) without the **evidence layer** every serious competitor leads with.
