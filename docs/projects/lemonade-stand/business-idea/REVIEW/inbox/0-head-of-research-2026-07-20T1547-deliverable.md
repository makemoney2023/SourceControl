## What we found

- **Evidence state (fact):** No research sources are uploaded yet; the venture rests on your one-line operator note only.
- **Category economics (assumption):** Fresh lemonade vendors often see strong gross margins on ingredients, but net profit after booth fees, labor, and travel is much thinner—**which event you pick matters more than the recipe**.
- **Differentiation (inference):** “Fresh-squeezed + ice-cold” is already standard at festivals; you likely need a second angle (flavors, speed, spectacle, niche events, or organizer deals) to stand out.
- **Regulatory (inference):** Adult-operated event stands usually need proper temporary food permits—child lemonade-stand exemptions typically do not apply.
- **Pricing cross-check (inference):** Finance modeled around ~$5/cup; common street/fair pricing runs **$6–8/cup**—higher prices improve break-even at paid events.
