# Deliverable — Phase 0 CMO peer brief

## In plain English
We mapped who the lemonade stand should sell to, how it should position, and which channels matter for a first season of events. The promise is ice-cold lemonade squeezed fresh at the event—not a permanent shop and not an ads-led brand. Season-one marketing is mostly the booth plus getting booked by organizers. Work is ready for the CEO to merge with finance, ops, and research; Phase 0 is not closed yet.

## What we found
- Primary customer (assumption): event attendees wanting a cold, fresh lemonade treat; secondary: organizers needing a simple drink vendor.
- Positioning (assumption): fresh + cold + occasion + honest simplicity — working line *Ice-cold lemonade, squeezed fresh at the event.*
- Channels (assumption): booth and organizer slots first; light signage/WOM next; paid ads and SEO deferred until economics are proven.
- No research sources are uploaded yet, so these are planning assumptions, not proven market facts.
- Soft flag: live squeezing sells the story but can slow the line — ops and marketing need to stay aligned.
