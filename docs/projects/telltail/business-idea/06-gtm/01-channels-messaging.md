### Surface CTAs

| Surface | CTA | Anti-CTA |
|---------|-----|----------|
| **Hero** | Film this moment / Finish one real scare | “See what your dog is thinking” / “Talk to your dog” |
| **Lite complete** | Continue with Plus — 60 honest reads | “Unlock unlimited AI” / paywall *before* the card |
| **Paywall primary** | Get Plus — $12/mo | “Compare scan counts” / “Same as Tailo” |
| **Paywall secondary** | Stay on Lite | Guilt about leftover credits |
| **Refuse** | Stop. Create space. Call a vet / credentialed trainer. | “You’re safe.” “Won’t bite.” “Try anyway.” |
| **Store (later)** | Lifestyle + Education listing; Get — 60 honest reads disclosed | Translator subtitle; $9.99; unlimited |
| **Zero remaining** | Credits or wait — refuse still on | “Skip this refuse” |

### Same-screen claims rules

Footer ToS does **not** un-say a chip. **[F]** US-06 / COO ladder

**On the same screen as any action card:**

- Observable signals + “likely,” never “is”
- Confidence visible
- 1–3 next-60-second actions *or* a refuse
- Stop-rule visible
- “Not a diagnosis. Not a safety clearance. Does not replace a veterinarian or trainer.”

**Auto-refuse (no action card) [F] AC-04.1:** kids-in-frame · snap / bite-risk · medical · confidence-floor fail.  
**Not auto-refuse:** freeze / whale-eye / stare — those are **gate inputs** (signals the gate reads). Ads must not say “if he freezes, we always refuse.” That would promise a gate the PRD does not ship, and Stage 0 would never card the job.

**A5 unnamed:** no public authority, no fake named expert, no anonymous PetGPT. Reward-based / management language only until founder locks a voice. **[F]**

**A10:** do not export Dogs Trust 80/24 into US copy. UK survey stays in research docs. **[F]**

**Cloud honesty:** video leaves the phone. Never “on-device.” **[F]** US-11
