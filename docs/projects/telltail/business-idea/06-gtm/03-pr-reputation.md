## Reputation thesis

Telltail earns the right to be talked about only as an **AI dog trainer** that is honest when it cannot help. The public tax on this category is already written. Kinship / Adopt-a-Pet (Marianne Eloise, 16 Apr 2026) quoted behaviorist Sarah Easterbrook calling Traini “a gimmick” and “potentially dangerous.” **[F]** Our first clip must not be the article they were answering. **[I]**

The reputation we want later: trainers can recommend us without using us as a cautionary slide; Kinship-class pet media can cover a *stop-rule*, not a talking tail. **[A]**

The reputation we refuse: another translator / collar / Entertainment toy. Never a Traini or PettiChat launch story. Never “relaxed / safe / won’t bite.” Never a fake expert. Never a launch date. **[F]** locks.

Honesty is the product in public. If Flash cannot refuse, there is no launch story. We withdraw the paywall. We do not PR our way out. **[F]** packet / strategy.

### Why this is the only honest wedge

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Training tool / AI dog trainer | Toy, translator, entertainment |
| Clip → 1–3 next actions, or a hard stop | “See what your dog is thinking” |
| Metered because a wrong trainer must not fire 200×/month | Unlimited Gemini at coffee-tier |
| Same *wallet* as Pupford / Zigzag | Same *shelf* as Traini |

A+C (moment coach gated by refuse-first) is a **test**, not a launch lock. **[A]** CEO recommended, not locked.

## Hostile-Q prep

Dry. One beat. No charm. Do not overclaim.

| Question | Answer | Do not |
|----------|--------|--------|
| **Sci Rep says AI can’t read dog faces. Why should anyone trust you?** | Agree. Martvel et al., *Scientific Reports*, 21 Nov 2025: general LVLMs (including the class we use) fall to near-chance on elicited dog faces; backgrounds drive labels. That is why refuse-first is the product, not a footnote. We will not ship a “relaxed / safe / won’t bite” chip. Flash-refuse is still an open eval. **[F]** | “We solved it.” Any accuracy %. |
| **Tailo / Aplexity are unlimited at about $10. Aren’t you stingy?** | They sell uncapped Gemini scans. We cap at 60 Flash reads so a wrong trainer does not fire 200 times a month. A refuse still happens at zero credits. This is our COGS + safety choice, not “what serious apps do.” **[F]** | “Serious apps meter.” Matching their $9.99 unlimited frame. |
| **Are you a translator?** | No. We are an AI dog trainer. We look at observable signals and suggest the next 60 seconds — or we refuse. We do not translate. We do not certify safe. If a screenshot looks like a dog quote, we failed. **[F]** | “Better translator.” “See what your dog is thinking.” |
| **Is this Telltail Dog Training in Little Rock?** | No. That is Elizabeth Silverstein’s force-free practice (`telltaildogtraining.com`). We are a different venture. We will not squat their phrase. If you meant them, here is their site. **[F]** | “Same family.” Buying their name. A fight. |
| **Did you mean Telltale Games?** | No. Misspelling. Not a story. **[F]** | A clever riff. |
