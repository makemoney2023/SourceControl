## AC-04.1 — content doctrine (do not flatten)

**[F]** `05-prd.md` AC-04.1: auto-refuse (no action card) fires only for **kids-in-frame**, **snap / bite-risk**, **medical**, or **confidence-floor fail**. Freeze / whale-eye / stare are **gate inputs** — signals the gate *reads* — not automatic refuse.

**What that means for titles and outlines:**

- **Do** teach freeze / whale-eye / hard stare as *observable signals* you notice, then pick the next 60 seconds (space, stop petting, end the greeting, or — if other risk is present — stop and call a human).
- **Do not** write “if you see whale eye we always refuse” as doctrine. That would empty the job: Stage 0 would never card a freeze-at-the-door scare. **[F]**
- **Do not** write “whale eye means relaxed” or “stare means safe.” Signals ≠ clearance. **[F]** claims ladder
