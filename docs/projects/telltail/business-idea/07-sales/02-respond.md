## Auto-replies

No form. No price field. No deposit. No “request a quote.” Stay on GTM CTAs. **[F]**

Use these as first-touch macros. A human may send the same words. A5 unnamed — no byline, no “your trainer,” no celebrity name.

### AR-0 — Safety (bite / kids / medical)

```
I understand this is scary, and I will not clear it from here.

Stop. Create space. If anyone is hurt or you think they will be, call a vet or a credentialed trainer / veterinary behaviorist. Telltail is not a diagnosis and does not replace them.

I will not say the dog is relaxed, safe, or “won’t bite.” If a child is in the room, we will not coach a next step that puts them closer.

If you want a moment card, attach a short clip in this thread. No clip means I did not see the dog — I will not pretend I did.
```

### AR-1 — Chat-described scare (media not yet attached)

```
Got it — you want the next 60 seconds, not a lecture.

I can take the story here (what happened, kids, food, doorbell, visitor). That is context. It is not a read.

When you want a moment card, attach a short clip or stills in this thread. Film this moment if you still can; if you missed it, upload what you have. The first Lite scare is meant to finish — card or a hard stop. We will not paywall you mid-scare.
```

### AR-2 — Chat-described scare (they asked for a card from text)

```
I have the story. I have not seen the dog.

Chat is for context and conversation. I will not turn a paragraph into a moment card, and I will not say I saw a signal I did not see.

If the job is a card, attach a clip in this thread. If the honest next step is already stop, skip the card: create space and call a vet or credentialed trainer.
```

### AR-3 — Translator anti-persona

```
We are an AI dog trainer, not a translator. I will not turn a bark into a quote or a sticker.

If you just had a scare and want the next right thing, tell me what happened and attach a clip. If you wanted “what is my dog saying,” this is the wrong app — and that is on purpose.
```

### AR-4 — Unlimited hunter

```
I understand sixty reads looks small next to an unlimited scan app.

We cap Flash so a wrong trainer cannot fire two hundred times a month. A refuse still happens at zero credits. That is a safety meter, not a stingy one.

You can stay on Lite (a few honest reads, gates on). If you are asking what Plus is *while the test is on*: $12/month or $99/year, same 60-read meter, not $9.99, not unlimited. If the test fails, we withdraw that paywall — we will not sell you a course at the same price.

What happened in the scare you actually care about?
```

### AR-5 — Bite-rehab self-serve

```
I understand you want this solved at home. I will not write a rehab protocol from this thread.

Stop. Create space. A bite history belongs with a veterinarian or a credentialed trainer / veterinary behaviorist. Telltail does not replace them and does not diagnose.

I will not sell you more reads as the fix.
```

### AR-6 — Billing / Plus (IAP exists later; explore copy only)

```
Plus is an in-app purchase — there is no pricing form and no deposit.

While the test is on: $12/month or $99/year. Sixty honest Flash reads, then credits. A read is a moment or a refuse. The quota cannot skip a bite-risk refuse, including at zero remaining. Never $9.99. Not unlimited.

If you already paid, restore purchases in the App Store (when a listing exists). If we withdraw the test, we withdraw this paywall. $12 is the listed price, not a promise that people will pay it.
```

### AR-7 — After-hours hold

```
We have your message. A person will reply in the support window ([Operator to set]).

If someone is in danger now: stop, create space, call a vet or emergency services. Do not wait on this thread for a safety clearance.
```

## Chat lane

| Rule | Do | Do not |

| Claims | Same bans as the card and the listing. Footer does not un-say a chat line. | relaxed / safe / won’t bite; translator; diagnose; unlimited; “what serious apps do.” |
| Escalate | Bite, pain, injury, kids-in-frame, “is he aggressive / safe for my kid?” → stop, space, vet / credentialed trainer. | In-app bite-rehab. “You’re safe.” |
| Voice | Reward-based / management. A5 unnamed. | Named expert. Anonymous PetGPT persona. Any celebrity trainer name. |
| K1 | If Flash cannot refuse, Plus is not a product. Chat does not paper that. | “We’re improving the model” as the answer to a scary clip. |
