## Qualification checklist

Run this **before** any Plus ask. A Lite scare that is still in progress is **not** a paywall moment (US-07 / AC-07.4).

### Lane gates — chat vs clip

Qualify the *input* first. Chat and clip are different jobs.

| Signal | Lane | Pass / fail | What Close may do | What Close must not do |
|--------|------|-------------|-------------------|------------------------|

Become anonymous PetGPT. |
| Clip or stills attached in-thread or via capture / upload | **Clip-ready moment** | **Pass for the gate.** Card is not guaranteed. | Run refuse-first → card **or** refuse. One cloud call. | Kids in frame, or owner marks kids present | Clip or chat | **Fail for a coaching card.** **Pass as refuse-as-close.** | Stop. Create space. Escalate to vet / credentialed trainer. | “Safe.” “Won’t bite.” “You’re fine to let the kid approach.” |

## Talk tracks

Voice: competent, calm, short. Evidence-led. No invented pricing law. No named trainer (A5 **OPEN**). Reward-based / management only.

**Close method (ethical adaptation):** disclose the hard parts *before* the Plus ask (60, refuse-at-zero, clip required for a card). Then one consent question. Stay-on-Lite is a real answer. Do not karate-refute “only 60.” Acknowledge, then ask what they actually need from the next scare. (YouTube standing context: Conversation Aikido + disclose-before-ask. Discard high-pressure / backup-close pressure.)

### 1) Lite finish (card)

**When:** Gate passed. Card is on screen. First scare completed.

**Say:**
> Here’s what we can see — not what he’s thinking. These are the signals, how sure we are, and one to three things to try in the next minute. If it gets worse, stop. We’re not a diagnosis and we’re not a safety clearance.

**Ask (optional, after they read the card):**
> Did that give you a next step you can actually do right now?

**If yes, and they ask what next:** stay on Lite, or — only if they qualify — the Plus-test track. **Do not** paywall over the card.

**If they want a streak / daily scan:**
> Keep is the next real scare, not a daily check-in. We don’t want a wrong coach firing for fun.

### 2) Refuse-as-close

**When:** Kids-in-frame, snap / bite-risk, medical, or confidence-floor fail. This *is* a finished Lite scare (US-07).

**Say:**
> We’re not going to coach this one. Stop. Create space. Call a veterinarian — and a credentialed trainer or veterinary behaviorist if this is a pattern. Telltail is not a diagnosis and does not replace them.

**Never say:** relaxed, safe, won’t bite, “try anyway,” “upgrade to see what we would have said,” “use a credit to skip this.”

**If they ask “but I paid / I will pay”:**
> A paid meter cannot buy a card we should not give. The gate still runs at zero reads. That’s the product.

**Escalate-to-human:** see below. This Close *ends* on the human, not on Plus.

### 3) Plus test (IAP — not a demo)

**When:** Lite-finish ready **and** Plus-test candidate. A+C still a test. K1 has not fired.

**Disclose first (same viewport as price, US-09):**
> Plus is sixty Flash reads a month, then credits. A read is one moment or a refuse. The gate still runs when you’re at zero — you cannot buy your way around a bite-risk refuse. This is not unlimited. We cap so a wrong trainer cannot fire two hundred times a month.

**Price (presentation, not WTP):**
> $12 a month, or $99 a year. Same sixty-read meter either way. Not $9.99. Annual is not unlimited-for-a-year.

**Consent ask:**
> If that meter makes sense as a safety cap — not as a scan fight — you can try Plus. If you wanted unlimited Gemini, this is the wrong app. You can stay on Lite.

**If they compare Tailo / Aplexity:** use objection **O1**, then re-ask consent. One time. Do not enter a scan-count cage match.

**If the test or K1 is withdrawn:** do **not** run this track. Do not swap in a $12 course.

**Internal kill line (sayable if asked):**
> If Flash cannot refuse, Plus is not a product. We don’t paper that with more credits or an accuracy number.

### 4) “I don’t have a video” (chat lane)

**When:** Chat-described scare. No clip, no stills.

**Say:**
> Tell me what just happened — who was in the room, food, doorbell, visitor, what you saw. I’ll use that as context. I cannot give you a moment card from text. I didn’t see the dog. When you can, attach a short clip or a still in this thread. Then we run the real gate: card or we refuse.

**Gather (do not diagnose from the answers):**
1. What happened in the last minute?
2. Kids or other dogs in the room?
3. Food, door, stranger, or body-handling?
4. Did anyone get mouthed or snapped at?
5. Do you have *any* clip or still from just now — even after the fact?

**If bite-risk / kids / medical comes through in chat with no media:**
> I’m not going to pretend I saw him. Stop, create space, call a vet / credentialed trainer. If you later attach a clip, we may still refuse — that’s allowed.

**If they demand a card from the paragraph:**
> That would be a chatbot guessing. We don’t do that. Context now, clip when you have one.

**Do not:** invent signals, name a feeling as fact, or say “he’s probably just [state].”
