## Summary

Telltail looks like a **training tool you notice**, not a toy you play. The name wears as *telltale signs* — a small, honest mark in a real kitchen or doorway — never as *talking-tail*. **[F]** PMM name wear + CD lock.

Visual system: photoreal rooms, observable body language, a next-action card, and a **visible refuse**. Quiet. Dry. A little literary. Never cartoon empathy. Never a green safety light. **[A]** this-pass look (inherits CD direction).

Stills this pass: **skipped.** Reason: explore · outlines only · no store / no paid. **[F]** Phase 10 proceed-to-creative + CD packet. `generation_used: none`. Cursor built-in image gen is not a complete.

Kids leftover is **not a stall** and is **not** a “no kids ever” brand. Kids-in-frame is a **product refuse** (we will not coach that clip), not a family-exclusion look. **[F]** Phase 10 / CD.

If a store ever exists: **Lifestyle + Education.** Never Entertainment. **[F]**

SKU optics if price appears: **$12/mo / $99/yr**, 60 Flash + credits. Never $9.99. Never unlimited. Meter = our COGS + safety, not “what serious apps do.” **[F]** Phase 4 / 10. $12 is presentation, not WTP (A4 OPEN). **[A]**

A5 named training voice stays **OPEN**. No Cesar. No anonymous PetGPT. No invented school. **[F]**

## Essence

**What the brand is:** the small sign you should have seen, and the next right thing — or the stop. **[I]**

**Mood words (use):** dry competence, quiet, literary, observational, kitchen-hour, doorway, calm under a scare. **[A]**

**Mood words (refuse):** playful, sticker, translator, fun, “deeper bond,” kennel cinema, show-ring, safety-green, celebrity trainer.

**Name wear [F]**

| Do | Do not |

| Disambiguate from Telltail Dog Training (Elizabeth Silverstein, Little Rock) | Buy or wear `telltail.com`; squat their exact brand phrase |
| Telltale *signs* | Telltale Games misspell as our job |

- **Never** encode safety as green. Confidence is Slate language (low / med / high or a number), never a green-to-red bar. **[F]** claims ladder

## Logo / mark (outline — no file this pass)

**[A]** craft. No Layer B mark rendered.

1. **Primary:** wordmark **Telltail** in Display (Newsreader), Ink on Paper. Letterspacing tight enough to read as one word; the double-l is the only “sign” — a hairline Sign tick *beside* the word, not a tail, not a speech bubble.
2. **Lockup:** wordmark + competence line in UI sans, Slate: `Dog training` or `Body language · next step`. Never `translator` / `what your dog is saying`.
3. **Icon (later):** a small observational mark (tick / notch / telltale), Ink or Sign on Paper. Not a dog face. Not a wagging tail. Not a quote sticker.
4. **Clear space:** one cap-height of the T around the wordmark.
5. **Minimum:** 88px digital wordmark width; icon 24px.

**Incorrect usage**

- Stretch, recolor to green, add a cartoon dog, put a speech bubble on a tail
- Wear Telltail Dog Training’s lockup or “Every Tail Has Its Tale”
- Place the wordmark on a still that looks like a translator screenshot
- Combine with Cesar / celebrity trainer face, Blacksage kennel grade, or Sieger show chrome

## Imagery / 3D rules

Photography is the brand. Illustration is the exception (icons only, hairline, no mascot).

### Photography **[A]** look, inheriting CD

| Rule | Do | Do not |
|------|----|--------|
| Place | Real kitchen, doorway, visitor threshold, hallway. Window or overhead kitchen light. | Studio seamless, agility ring, kennel run, show stack |
| Subject | One dog + adult owner. Observable body (weight shift, freeze, mouth, visitor at the jamb). | Speech-bubble dog, celebrity trainer, “relaxed” grin-to-camera |
| Kids | Household *context* may exist in a room. Brand is not “no kids ever.” **[F]** | Child as the coaching subject; “safe with kids” hero; green-light family poster |
| Action | Calm under a scare. Phone raised or just-raised. Next-action card or refuse in frame as UI, not as a sticker on the dog. | Traini quote sticker, bark-to-English, fun montage |
| Grade | Photoreal documentary. Natural skin/fur. Brand hex only as a practical (Sign under a lamp, Ink shadows) — not a color wash. | Purple glow, cream-terracotta AI default, Blacksage void, CGI sheen |
| Crop | Doorway 3:4 or 4:5; kitchen 16:9 hero; UI still 3:2. Leave CTA-safe right/bottom third. | Face-only dog crop that reads as a translator avatar |

### 3D **[A]**

- **No 3D dog. No mascot. No talking-tail sculpture.**
- If Phase 12 ever needs a product ref: the **phone** as a still-life (glass, aluminum, Paper table, kitchen wood). Photoreal materials. Not a character.
- No img2threejs dog. Kennel / show 3D is out of venture.

### Icons

- Hairline 1.5px, Ink or Slate, 24px grid, corner 2px.
- Motifs: doorway, kitchen light, stop rule (a bar, not a red octagon toy), clip, refuse.
- No paw-print as the logo. No bone. No wagging-tail spinner.

## UI motifs

These are the brand objects Phase 12 must draw. Not wireframes — motif locks.

### Moment card

- Paper field, Hairline edge, 16–20px radius (quiet, not pill-toy).
- Stack, top → bottom: **observable signals** (UI sans) → **confidence** (Mono, Slate; low/med/high or a number — never a green bar) → **1–3 next-60s actions** (numbered, verb-led) → **stop-rule** (Display or UI medium, Ink).
- One Sign tick (8×8 or a 2px rule) as the telltale — not a dog sticker.
- No quote. No “what the dog is thinking.” No 6-week plan.

### Refuse card

- **First-class surface**, not a toast under a coaching card. **[F]** US-03
- Paper or Field ground; Refuse as the title rule / word “Stop” or “We will not coach this clip.”
- Body stays Ink. Escalate line in Slate: space now; vet / DACVB / credentialed trainer. Not a diagnosis.
- Kids-in-frame refuse: same card, no child identity, no retained kid frame as the hero still. **[F]** US-04
- Quota cannot skip this card. Do not style it as a paywall upsell.

### No quote sticker

- There is no dog-quote component. If a screenshot would read as Traini, it failed. **[F]**

### Paywall (if drawn later)

- Hero is harm-per-wrong-fire + holding line. 60 + refuse-cannot-skip + no unlimited. **[F]** US-09
- SKU `$12` / `$99`. Never `$9.99`.

### Chat

- Thread on Field. Text is context. A vision card appears only after media is attached. No “I saw your dog” from a paragraph. **[F]** US-21
- Chat chrome is not a ghost trainer avatar (A5 OPEN).

### H1 — Kitchen moment (16:9)

```text
An adult in a real US kitchen at night, standing still a few feet from a medium mixed-breed dog whose weight is shifted back, mouth closed, eyes on the person's hands. Linoleum and oak cabinets, a ceiling fixture and a leftover dinner plate on the counter. The person holds an iPhone at chest height as if they just started filming. Observable body language only; no caption on the dog. Shot on a Canon EOS R6, 35mm at f/2.8, Kodak Portra 400, three-quarter doorway-to-kitchen framing, shallow depth on the dog's shoulders, background readable as a kitchen.
```

### H2 — Doorway / visitor (4:5)

```text
A front doorway from inside a modest house, afternoon window light. A dog stands at the jamb, body sideways, watching a visitor's shoes on the porch; the owner is a half-step back, one hand on the door, phone in the other. No child in the coaching foreground. Wood trim, storm door glass, a coat hook. Shot on a Leica Q2, 28mm at f/2.8, documentary color, deep enough DOF to read the threshold and the dog's weight shift.
```

### H3 — Visible refuse (3:2 UI still)

```text
Over-the-shoulder of an iPhone in a kitchen, the screen showing a quiet paper-colored card with a thin oxblood #6B2C28 rule and short instructional type, the card clearly a stop / we-will-not-coach surface, not a cartoon quote. The dog is out of focus beyond the phone, still in the room. Shot on an iPhone 15 Pro, 24mm equivalent, available overhead light, photoreal device glass and fingerprints, no sticker UI, no green badge.
```
