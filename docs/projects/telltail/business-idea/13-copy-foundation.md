### Moment (sample fill — doorway freeze, gate input, not refuse)

- **Signals:** Weight shifted back. Mouth closed. Eyes on the visitor’s shoes.
- **Likely:** Unsure (medium). Not a diagnosis.
- **1.** Step between the dog and the door. **2.** Ask the visitor to wait on the porch. **3.** Let him move away on his own.
- **Stop if** he lunges, shows teeth, or you cannot make space.
- **Caption:** Not a diagnosis. Not a safety clearance. Does not replace a veterinarian or trainer. This clip left your phone.

### Refuse — bite-risk / snap

- **Title:** We will not coach this clip.
- **Body:** Stop. Create space. Get people out of reach.
- **Escalate:** Call your vet or a credentialed trainer (DACVB / CAAB / CPDT). We will not name a diagnosis from a phone clip.
- **No:** Try anyway. You’re safe. Won’t bite.

### Refuse — kids-in-frame

- **Title:** We will not coach this clip.
- **Body:** A child is in this frame. We won’t treat them as the training subject.
- **Escalate:** Separate child and dog. If anyone was touched, call your vet. AVMA: never leave a baby or small child alone with a dog.
- **No:** Family-exclusion brand. “Safe with kids.” A leftover stall.

### Refuse — floor fail (not enough)

- **Title:** We will not coach this clip.
- **Body:** Not enough here to pick a next step we can stand behind.
- **Next:** Another angle, or stop and give space. We will not guess.
