### 2.2 Pros / cons / risks per option

#### A — Moment coach (alone)

- **Pros:** Closest to founder words; only structural difference vs Doris / Zigzag / ChatGPT is the camera **[I]**; iOS-first clip form is the supply-side hole Pawfessor occupies without refuse.
- **Cons:** A wrong “do this now” is worse than a wrong sticker (Sci Rep + Kinship) **[F]**; 60-read meter looks stingy next to Aplexity / Tailo unlimited unless C is the reason; film-live is OPEN.
- **Risks:** Ships as Tailo-with-tips; inherits Traini gimmick tax on one lazy subtitle; Flash misfire on freeze / whale-eye becomes the launch review.

#### B — Curriculum trainer

- **Pros:** Proven $9–13 wallet **[F]**; easiest COGS; survives film-live = false; lowest Traini confusion; vision can stay optional / credit-gated.
- **Cons:** Dilutes the founder’s live-second job; we become “another course app”; camera (the only wedge vs Doris / ChatGPT) is demoted.
- **Risks:** Competes with Pupford / Zigzag on their field; annual $79 is weak if any frontier vision is bundled; does not answer “can Flash refuse?” because it mostly does not ask.

#### C — Refuse-first trainer (alone)

- **Pros:** Strongest safety stance on a Flash stack; opposite of a translator; matches COO kill-shot + CMO “know when to stop”; meter is easier to defend as “we will not fire a wrong trainer 200×.”
- **Cons:** Can feel like a safety product, not a trainer; instruction is conditional; unproven WTP for denial; still needs a clip for the instruction half.
- **Risks:** High refuse rate → churn / refunds (unproven); if Flash cannot hold a floor, C is a product that mostly says stop — $9 Plus still dies (I4).

#### A gated by C — CEO recommended *test* form (not a BA lock)

- **Pros:** Keeps the founder job (A) and the only honest claims stance on this stack (C); is the only form where a 60-read meter has a shopper-legible reason next to Aplexity $9.99 unlimited and Tailo Pro £8.99 unlimited **[I]**; refuse-first + iOS moment cards is still a **supply gap**, not demand proof **[I]**.
- **Cons:** Stacks A’s film-live OPEN on C’s Flash-refuse OPEN; two load-bearing unproven bets in one test; shopper still compares 60 reads to unlimited Gemini peers.
- **Risks:** If Flash cannot refuse, $9 Plus is not a product (do not open a prompt bake-off). If owners will not film the scare, the A half is after-action homework. If they *will* pay $9–13, they may still pick unlimited Tailo / Aplexity unless refuse is the hero of the paywall. **BA does not pick this.** CEO recommended it as the *test to carry*. Compare it; do not lock it.

**No pick.** This draft will not smuggle a winner. CEO may still carry A+C, pick B, pick A-alone, pick C-alone, or hold explore.
