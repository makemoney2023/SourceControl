## One-sentence idea

Telltail is a consumer mobile app that uses LLM vision on a phone camera clip to interpret a dog’s body language in the moment, then returns short, doable training next-steps — not a novelty “what is my dog saying” sticker. **[A]** working product shape from founder words.

## Problem

Dog owners constantly see behavior they cannot decode. They treat stress as stubbornness, fear as “being bad,” arousal as play, and resource guarding as greed. The wrong next move makes the dog worse and sometimes unsafe. **[I]**

Trainers cannot sit in the living room. Existing help is generic or delayed. A cluster of “AI dog translator” apps labels emotion but rarely owns the instruction and often overclaims. **[I]** Category existence **[F]** (public pages 2026-08-21).

## Who feels it

- P1: reactive / adolescent owners. **[A]**

Who pays v1: B2C owner. **[A]**

## Proposed solution

Clip/stills → vision read of signals + owner context → (1) what we see (2) likely state + confidence (3) 1–3 actions for the next 60 seconds (4) escalate to vet/behaviorist when medical or bite-risk. Light history for a trainer. **[A]**

Wedge: competitors sell empathy; Telltail sells competence (what to do now, and when to stop). **[A]**
