## What this is

Telltail is a training tool for the scare you’re in. One chat. One thread.

You tell what just happened. You attach a clip. The app looks at what you can see — weight, mouth, where the eyes go — and gives you 1–3 things to do in the next 60 seconds. Or it stops, and tells you to make space and call a human.

It is not a translator. It will not tell you what your dog is thinking. It will not say you are safe.

The same app in the browser (installable PWA) and on your phone (Ionic Capacitor wrap). Not three products. Not iOS-only. No App Store or Play listing this pass. **[F]**

---

## The next 60 seconds

The jump, the mouth, the freeze at the jamb — it already happened. Hugging him, correcting him, crowding him can practice the scare.

One doable next step. Then the stop-rule: stop if he lunges, shows teeth, or you cannot make space.

YouTube and a class next Tuesday are not *this* dog, *this* second. Neither is a cartoon quote on his face.

## What we will not do

- Translate the dog. Bark-to-English. “See what your dog is thinking.”
- Call a freeze, whale-eye, or stare an automatic refuse. Those are things we look at. **[F]** AC-04.1
- Say relaxed, safe, or won’t bite.
- Replace your vet or a credentialed trainer.
- Sell a 6-week course at $12 if the moment-coach test fails.
- Pretend this is three apps, or an iPhone-only store listing.
