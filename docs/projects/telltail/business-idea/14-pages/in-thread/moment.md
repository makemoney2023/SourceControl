## Sample fill — doorway freeze (gate input, not auto-refuse)

AC-04.1: freeze / whale-eye / stare are **inputs**. This card is legal. Do not retitle it as a refuse. **[F]**

**Eyebrow:** Next 60 seconds.

**We see** weight shifted back, mouth closed, eyes on the visitor’s shoes.

**Likely** unsure (medium). Not a diagnosis.

**Try this.**
1. Step between the dog and the door.
2. Ask the visitor to wait on the porch.
3. Let him move away on his own.

**Stop if** he lunges, shows teeth, or you cannot make space.
