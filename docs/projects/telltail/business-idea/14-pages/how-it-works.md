## Three beats

Not a syllabus. Not six weeks.

### 1. Tell the scare

Empty thread: *What just happened?*

The doorway. The mouth on your hands. The growl over the bowl. Write it like you’d text a person in the next room. After the moment is fine — a still can still become a card or a refuse. **[A]** A1 still OPEN

### 2. Attach a clip

Camera or Photos, from the same composer. A vision card does **not** appear from text alone. We will not say we already saw your dog.

Before you send: this clip leaves your phone. **[F]** US-11

### 3. A card — or a refuse — in the thread

**If we can stand behind a next step:** signals you can see, a *likely* state with confidence, 1–3 actions for the next 60 seconds, and a stop-rule on the same card.

**If we cannot:** we will not coach this clip. Stop. Create space. Call a vet or a credentialed trainer. That refuse is first-class, not a toast, not an upsell.

Lite (free) is 3–5 reads. The first scare must finish — card *or* refuse. We will not paywall the card. **[F]** US-07

## What a freeze is (and is not)

A freeze, a whale-eye, a stare — those are things we look at. They are **not** an automatic stop. **[F]** AC-04.1

If the honest next step is space, the card will say so. If the clip is bite-risk, medical, a child in frame, or too thin to stand behind, we refuse. Those are different doors.

Kids leftover is an **in-thread refuse**, not a page on this site and not “no families.” We do not write that card here. **[F]** Content lease
