## Product Overview

**One-liner:** See the signal. Do the next right thing — and know when to stop.

**What it does:** Telltail is a consumer iOS app that takes a phone-camera clip, runs one Flash-class multimodal LLM read, and returns 1–3 doable actions for the next ~60 seconds — plus a hard stop when the honest next step is *stop*. It is a refuse-first moment coach, not a 6-week curriculum and not a “what is my dog saying” sticker. **[A]** product form lock

**Product category (shelf the buyer searches):** dog training app / body-language training tool. App Store: **Lifestyle + Education**. Never Entertainment. Never Games. Never “translator.” **[F]** `02-keyword-demand.md` ASO lock

**Product type:** B2C consumer mobile (iOS-first). Cloud multimodal read per clip — not a custom dog-pose detector. **[F]** stack lock

**Business model (packaging lock, not a WTP finding):**

| Tier | What we sell | What we do not say |
|------|----------------|--------------------|
| **Free / Lite** | 3–5 Lite reads — enough to finish *one real scare* and see the stop-rule | “Full AI.” Unlimited try. Frontier model. |
| **Plus ($9–13/mo)** | **60 Flash-class reads/mo + credits.** Quota cannot skip bite-risk refuse. Disclose 60 + credits on the **paywall and the listing**. | “Unlimited.” “What serious apps do.” “Ask as often as you want.” |
| **Credits** | Extra Flash-class reads after the 60. Leftover credits are a feature, not a guilt trip. | Credits as a way around refuse. |
| **Trainer seat** | Later. Channel, not v1 buyer. | v1 SKU. “Replaces your trainer.” |

Annual band from intake ($79–99/yr) is a **CFO seed, not a messaging lock**. Phase 4 owns price presentation. **[A]**

**Metering story (load-bearing).** Metering is **our COGS + safety choice**, not a category norm. **[F]** Phase 2 C-suite: Aplexity Pro = unlimited Gemini scans at $9.99; Tailo Pro = unlimited Gemini in the $9–13 band (£8.99/mo). Shoppers will hear “stingy” next to those two unless refuse-first is the *reason* for the cap. **Do not** market 60 as “what serious apps do.” Market it as how a **wrong trainer does not fire 200×/month**. **[F]** packet

## Positioning

### Category we sit in

**AI dog trainer / refuse-first moment coach.** Same *wallet* as Pupford and Zigzag (paid training help). Different *job*: this dog, this second — not a syllabus. **[I]**

We do **not** sit in the 24-year “dog translator” toy lineage (BowLingual 2002 → Traini / Entertainment clones). Buying that shelf purchases junk intent and the Kinship trainer tax. **[F]**

### Positioning statement

For **first-time and anxious US/CA dog owners** who just missed a live moment (jump, mouth, freeze, growl, visitor) and do not know the next 60 seconds, **Telltail** is the **refuse-first moment coach** that turns a phone clip into 1–3 next actions — and tells them when to stop. Unlike Gemini-wrapper “emotion” apps (Pawfessor, Tailo, Aplexity) and unlike unlimited curriculum (Pupford / Zigzag), Telltail leads with **observable signals + a stop-rule**, meters Flash reads so a wrong coach cannot fire 200 times a month, and never claims to read a dog’s mind.

### The wedge we can own

*This dog, this second → 1–3 actions + when to stop and call a human.* **[A]** CMO Phase 0, still the working wedge

We are **not** the first vision reader. We are the first (if product holds) to treat the read as a **training prompt with a safety gate**. **[I]**

Empty cell on the Phase 2 map **[I]:** iOS-live clip → 60-second competence cards + refuse-first bite/vet gate + non-translator ASO + metered Flash (60/mo; quota cannot skip refuse). Pieces exist separately (Pawfessor iOS + credits; Tailo next-step / unlimited Pro; PetSignalAI web triage). Nobody publishes the intersection.

## Claims tiers

### Tier 1 — verifiable (allowed)

Say these only when the product actually does them.

| “Helps you notice body-language signals and pick a next step” | Education / competence. Hard-stop + “not a vet / not a translator” in **listing copy**, not only ToS. |
| Plus includes 60 Flash-class reads/mo, then credits | Must appear on paywall + listing. |
| Reward-based methods only | AVSAB 2021. True of prompt + filter. |
| iOS-first; a read sends the clip to our processors | Cloud stack. Purpose string must match. |
| Kids-in-frame → we will not coach that clip | Product control. |

### Tier 2 — qualified (allowed-if-on-the-same-screen)

The only honest output shape: **“We see [observable signal]; likely [state] (confidence); try [1–3 actions]; stop if [X].”**

Qualifications that must be **on the same screen**:

- Observable signals only; “likely,” never “is”
- Numeric or low/med/high confidence
- Actions limited to the next ~60 seconds
- Hard-stop line visible
- “Not a diagnosis. Not a safety clearance. Does not replace a veterinarian or veterinary behaviorist (DACVB).”
- Refuse the card entirely below the confidence floor, or on bite-risk / medical / kids-in-frame

“Share a clip + notes with *your* trainer” — owner-initiated, separate consent, no implied endorsement, no re-score after a hard stop.

### Tier 3 — forbidden

| Claim | Why |
|-------|-----|
| Translator / “what your dog is saying” / “see what your dog is thinking” / bark-to-English / cartoon quotes | Unsubstantiated mind-reading. Category we refuse. |
| “Relaxed” / “safe to approach” / “won’t bite” / green safety light | Material safety claim. Kill-shot liability. Sci Rep + Kinship failure mode. |
| Diagnoses or treats anxiety, pain, illness; names a disease or drug | Practice of veterinary medicine. Escalate-don’t-diagnose. |
| “Replaces your trainer / vet / behaviourist” | FTC professional-substitution (DoNotPay analog). Tailo/Dog Advise copy we must not echo. |
| Outcome / efficacy: “stops reactivity,” “cures,” “fixes aggression” | Needs trials we do not have. |
| Accuracy %, “78% vs vet,” calmness 0–100 as a safety score | Como/Pawfessor anti-patterns. No published methodology. |
| “On-device” / “never leaves your phone” | v1 is cloud multimodal. False privacy claim. |

## Differentiation

**Key differentiators (only if product ships them):**

1. Refuse-first as the hero, not a toast under a calmness score.
2. 1–3 next-60-second actions on iOS, not a feeling label.
3. Metered Flash (60 + credits) framed as **safety + COGS**, disclosed, unable to skip bite-risk refuse.
4. Non-translator ASO (Lifestyle + Education; problem language).
5. Reward-based only; escalate-don’t-diagnose; kids-in-frame refuse.

**How we do it differently:** the read is a *training prompt with a gate*, not an empathy sticker and not a syllabus.

**Why that’s better:** a wrong confident label is the harm trainers already named (Kinship 2026). Competence *is* knowing when not to answer. **[I]**

**Why a customer would choose us:** they want to get through the next minute without making it worse — and they want the app to shut up when it should. **[I]** Unproven until interviews.

**Why a customer would not:** Tailo/Aplexity look like more app for the same $10; Doris/ChatGPT are already in their pocket; they will not raise the phone during the incident; Sci Rep makes a confident camera suspect. **[I]**

## Offer

From `offer-extraction`. Outcomes > features. Specific and believable — no hype.

**Offer angles**

1. **The next 60 seconds.** When the moment is already happening, get 1–3 things to do (or stop) before you make it worse.
2. **The stop-rule.** A trainer that knows when to shut up — refuse on bite-risk, pain, kids-in-frame. Quota cannot skip that.
3. **Moments, not a binge.** 60 Flash reads/mo so a wrong coach cannot fire 200 times. Credits if you need more. Not unlimited Gemini.

**Dominant offer:** *A refuse-first moment coach for the scare you’re in — 60 honest Flash reads a month, and a hard stop when the next right thing is to stop.*

**Why this, why now:** text-AI and YouTube already own “help in my pocket.” The remaining job is *this dog, this second*. The category that tried to own it sold stickers and unlimited Gemini. Trainers already called that dangerous. We sell the gate.

**Reciprocity asset (later, not a translator teaser):** a free *observable signals* checklist you can use without the app. Authority, not a lead-magnet toy. **[I]** CMO Phase 0

**Conversion moment:** scary clip → observable signals → 1–3 actions → escalate if needed → owner does *one* thing, or the app tells them to stop. Marketing owns the promise; product owns the false-relaxed miss; legal owns claims.

**Measurement (assumption, explore):** first-read completion, action-taken, “this helped / this was wrong,” escalate rate, D7 return on a *new* moment. Not translator-session vanity.
