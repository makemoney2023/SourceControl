## Summary

Telltail is an **AI dog trainer** for missed training moments: a phone clip becomes 1–3 next actions — and a hard stop when the honest next step is stop. We will **test** (not lock) a moment coach **gated by refuse-first**. We meter 60 Flash reads because a wrong trainer must not fire 200 times a month, not because “serious apps meter.” Flash-refuse is still **open**. Explore only.

**Line to hold:** *See the signal. Do the next right thing — and know when to stop.*

## Strategic thesis

If first-time and anxious owners will film (or immediately upload) a live scare, and if a Flash-class multimodal read can **refuse** when it cannot hold a confidence floor, then a metered iOS moment coach can take the *job* already paid to Pupford / Zigzag / ChatGPT and add the only thing those substitutes lack: **this dog, this second** — without becoming Traini. **[A]**

If either bet fails, $9 Plus is not a product. We do not prompt our way out. **[I]**

## Positioning / ICP / messaging

Full reusable brief: `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

**Category:** AI dog trainer / refuse-first moment coach. Wallet = Pupford / Zigzag. Not the translator shelf.

**Positioning:** For first-time and anxious US/CA owners who just missed a live moment, Telltail turns a phone clip into 1–3 next actions — and tells them when to stop. Unlike Gemini wrappers (Pawfessor, Tailo, Aplexity) and unlike unlimited curriculum, we lead with observable signals + a stop-rule, and we cap Flash reads so a wrong coach cannot fire 200×/month.

**ICP v1 [A]:** P0 first-week / first-time panic (provisional; 0 interviews). P1 reactive / adolescent. P2 trainers = channel. Anti: translator shoppers, unlimited-AI bargain hunters, bite-rehab self-serve, show/kennel.

**ASO:** Lifestyle + Education. Never Entertainment / translator.
