## Pricing posture

### Published vs inquiry-first

| Posture | Fit for Telltail v1 | Verdict |
|---------|---------------------|---------|
| **Published band + IAP paywall** | B2C iOS owner app. Apple lists IAP prices. P0 is an 11pm kitchen scare, not a kennel inquiry. | **Recommended.** **[I]** |
| **Inquiry-first / “request pricing”** | Right for Blacksage-style placements. Wrong friction for a $9–13 consumer clip app. | **Do not use on v1 Plus.** |
| **Unlisted / “coming soon” tease** | Explore mode may delay the *listing*, not the *honesty of the meter* once a paywall exists. | If a TestFlight paywall ships, it still discloses 60 + credits. |

**Band we may publish (seed, not a SKU lock):** Plus **$9–13/mo** or **$79–99/yr**. Lite 3–5 reads free. Credits after the 60. Trainer seat **$29–49/mo later, out of v1.** **[F]** packet / Phase 0 seed

**Do not invent** the exact $x.99 in this sidecar. FP&A / CFO pick the SKU inside the band. See contradiction C1 below for a presentation constraint on **$9.99 specifically**.

### What “published” must show

On **paywall and App Store listing** (listing copy when we are public; paywall as soon as IAP exists):

1. Plus = **60 Flash-class reads/mo + credits**
2. A read = one clip-in honest moment (or a refuse) — not unlimited chat
3. Quota **cannot skip** bite-risk refuse
4. Lite = 3–5 reads; safety gates always on
5. No “unlimited,” no “ask as often as you want,” no “what serious apps do”

## Package names and what’s included

v1 shows **two plans + an overflow**, not a three-tier “Good-Better-Best” that implies volume. Paradox of choice: trainer seat off the v1 paywall. **[I]** marketing-psychology

First Lite read must complete. | Frontier model. Unlimited try. “Full AI.” | Default. Reciprocity, not a mid-moment tease. |
| **Plus** | Plus | A month of moments that matter | **60 Flash-class cloud reads/mo + credits.** Frontier **cascade-only** (not a Plus entitlement). Meter disclosed. | Unlimited scans. Curriculum library. Human coach. Trainer seat. | Hero paid SKU. |
| **Credits** | Credits (overflow) | Extra moments after a hard week | Purchased Flash-class reads after the 60. Seed anatomy **$8–12 / 20 reads [A]** Phase 0 — **not a lock.** | A refuse bypass. ## Value metric

**Unit we sell:** a **read** = one honest moment.

| Is a read | Is not a read |
|-----------|----------------|
| One clip in → one card (1–3 next-60-second actions) **or** one refuse | An unlimited chat thread |
| A refuse that still happens at 0 credits | A scan we fire to “use up” the meter |
| This dog, this second | A 6-week lesson unlock |
| Flash-class cloud multimodal call (Plus) | On-device / “never leaves your phone” |

**Narrative:** You open Telltail when it matters. Sixty times a month is a lot of scares; two hundred is a binge a wrong trainer should not get. **[I]**

**Mental accounting (allowed):** “sixty moments” not “sixty API calls.” **Forbidden:** “less than a coffee” next to Aplexity’s $9.99 unlimited — that fight is on scan count, which we lose on purpose.

## Paywall presentation

CEO lock: **paywall must lead with harm-per-wrong-fire, not “unlimited?”** **[F]** `03-strategy.md` / Phase 3 C-suite

### Hero (Plus)

**Headline job:** *Sixty honest reads. A hard stop when the next right thing is to stop.*

**Subhead job:** *We cap Flash so a wrong trainer cannot fire two hundred times a month. A refuse still happens at zero credits.*

**Not the hero:** scan-count comparison, “same price as Tailo,” checkmarks for “AI,” a 0–100 calmness demo, a talking-tail.

### Layout (copy hierarchy)

1. **Loss / stop-rule** — what a false “relaxed” costs (kid, visitor, growl you punish). Pratfall/honesty: we will not certify safe. **[I]** marketing-psychology
2. **What you get** — 60 Flash reads/mo + credits; 1–3 actions or a refuse; kids-in-frame refuse; escalate, don’t diagnose.
3. **What 60 is** — moments that matter, not a binge. Meter **disclosed in the same viewport as the price**.
4. **Price** — published number inside $9–13/mo or $79–99/yr once CFO locks the SKU. Annual still meters 60/mo (it is not unlimited-for-a-year).
5. **Lite remainder** — “Try one scare free (3–5 Lite reads). Safety gates stay on.”

### CTA

| Surface | CTA | Anti-CTA |
|---------|-----|----------|
| Lite complete | Continue with Plus — 60 honest reads | “Unlock unlimited AI” |
| Paywall primary | Get Plus | “Compare scan counts” |
| Paywall secondary | Stay on Lite | Guilt about leftover credits |
| Zero remaining | Buy credits **or** wait for next month — refuse still on | “Skip this refuse” |

**Fogg:** first Lite read = one clip, one screen, three actions or a refuse. If Lite dies mid-scare, the paywall inherits a chargeback. **[I]**

**Default effect:** Plus is the only paid *plan* on the sheet. Credits are a button, not a column. **[I]**

## Presenting 60 next to Aplexity / Tailo unlimited

**Facts we do not reopen [F]** (`02-competitive-landscape.md`):

- Aplexity Pro = **unlimited Gemini scans at $9.99/mo**
- Tailo Pro = **unlimited Gemini** at **£8.99/mo** (in the $9–13 band)
- Pawfessor meters via credits ($0.99 / $3.99 / $7.99)
- Pupford / Zigzag ~$9.99 unlimited *content* / human chat — zero vision COGS

**Reference price in the shopper’s head is unlimited Gemini at ~$10.** Status-quo + anchoring. We do not win by matching volume. We **change the reference** from scans to **harm per wrong fire**. **[I]** prospect theory / buying-psychology

| Move | Line | Why |
|------|------|-----|
| **Yes — reframe** | “They sell uncapped Gemini. We cap so a wrong trainer does not fire 200×/month.” | Honesty + Sci Rep constraint |
| **Yes — refuse is the product** | “A refuse still happens at zero credits. Safety is not a premium feature.” | Meter ≠ skip-the-gate |
| **Yes — different job vs Pupford/Zigzag** | “They train the week. We coach the minute.” | Same wallet, not a scan fight |
| **No — category-norm** | “Serious apps meter at 60.” | **False.** Tailo Pro and Aplexity unlimit in-band. **[F]** |
| **No — stingy apology** | “Sorry we only give 60.” | Trains the unlimited review |
| **No — same-SKU cage match** | Plus listed at **$9.99** next to Aplexity $9.99 unlimited | Identical left digit, fewer scans = stingy. See C1. |
| **No — comparison ads** | “Same price as Pupford, unlimited help” | They sell curriculum; we do not |

**If the shopper’s only question is “why not unlimited?”** the paywall already lost. Lead with the scare and the stop-rule; let 60 be the *consequence* of honesty, not the headline number.

## Credit-pack offer anatomy

Phase 0 seed: **$8–12 for 20 extra Flash-class reads. [A] — not a lock.** FP&A prices COGS; this is merchandising shape only.

| Rule | Why |
|------|-----|
| Credits are **overflow**, not a plan | A third column becomes “buy more scans” and re-opens the Tailo fight |
| Do not sell credits as a refuse bypass | Quota cannot skip bite-risk refuse **[F]** |
| 20 reads ≈ one hard week, not a binge pack | A 200-read pack would mock the 200× safety line |
| Leftover credits = feature, not guilt | CMO Phase 0 |
| Show credits **after** Plus is understood | Hero paywall = Lite vs Plus only |
| Never “unlimited with credits” | Credits are finite reads |

Exact pack price, stack rank, and whether annual Plus includes a credit buffer stay with FP&A. **[A]**

## Offer anatomy (presentation)

From `offer-extraction`. Outcomes > features. No hype.

**Dominant offer (test, not a launch lock):**  
*A refuse-first moment coach — 60 honest Flash reads a month, credits if a week runs hot, and a hard stop when the next right thing is to stop.*

**Why this, why now:** text-AI and YouTube already own “help in my pocket.” Unlimited Gemini at $9–13 already owns scan volume. The remaining honest offer is *this dog, this second*, metered so a wrong coach cannot fire 200×/month.

**Kill switch inside the offer (must be sayable on the paywall if it fires):**  
If Flash **cannot refuse**, **$9 Plus is not a product.** Do not paper it with “we’re working on accuracy,” a calmness score, or more credits. The Plus promise is withdrawn; explore kill or human-in-the-loop — not a better prompt. **[F]** packet / `03-strategy.md`

**Annual:** $79–99/yr seed **[A]**. Presentation: still **60 Flash reads per month**, billed yearly. Never “unlimited for a year.” Show monthly equivalent only *after* the 60-read line, not instead of it.
