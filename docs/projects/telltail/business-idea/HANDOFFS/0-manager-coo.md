- **“Relaxed / safe to approach / won’t bite” is the kill-shot. [I]** FTC deception + substantiation doctrine treats a material safety implication as needing a reasonable basis *before* it ships; a buried disclaimer does not cure a confident UI label. Competitor pages already sell “translator / exactly how they feel / behaviorist in your pocket” — that is the overclaim pattern we must not copy.
- **Kids-in-frame is a day-one privacy gate, not a later polish. [F]** COPPA treats a photo, video, or audio file containing a child’s image or voice as personal information. **CFO lock:** the clip leaves the device on every v1 read, so the COPPA “on-device, never transmitted” exception does **not** apply. Cloud vision without a refuse/blur path + short retention + no training-on-user-video default creates COPPA / PIPEDA / BIPA / CPRA exposure.
- **Hard-stop language must escalate, not diagnose. [F]** California B&P §4826 and Virginia §54.1-3800 reserve diagnosis and treatment of animal disease to licensed veterinarians. ACVB: only a DACVB may call themselves a veterinary behaviorist. Allowed output is “stop; see a veterinarian / DACVB”; forbidden is “your dog has [condition]” or “treat with [X].”
- **Day-one ops is a metered cloud multimodal call plus a safety gate. [F]** CFO lock 2026-08-21: each read = clip in → cloud LLM → instruction out. Video leaves the device on v1. Safety classifier, aversive-method blocklist, hard-stop SLA, trainer-history retention, and a **metered Plus** quota (**60 Flash-class cloud reads/mo, then credits**) are production path, not Phase 8 leftovers. Quota must not weaken the bite-risk hard stop.

## Claims ladder

| Claim | Tier | Why |
|-------|------|-----|
| “Translates what your dog is saying” | **Forbidden** | Unsubstantiated mind-reading. FTC deception + substantiation: no reasonable basis that a clip becomes language. Competitor pattern we must not copy (Dogly: “Your dog is talking”; Traini: “real-time emotion translations”; Adopt-a-Pet 2026-04-16 critique: false certainty is the harm). PetSignalAI’s public anti-pattern is the one to steal: signals, not translations. |
| “Relaxed” / “safe to approach” / “won’t bite” | **Forbidden** | Material safety claim. A reasonable owner will act on it (approach, let a child approach, keep the food bowl down). No competent and reliable evidence that a phone clip can certify safety. Disclaimer does not cure the chip. Kill-shot liability. |
| “Diagnoses” / “treats” anxiety, pain, illness | **Forbidden** | Practice of veterinary medicine (CA B&P §4826; VA §54.1-3800). Also Apple 1.4.1 / medical-device declaration trap. DoNotPay analog: do not claim to substitute for a licensed professional. |
| “Replaces your trainer / vet” | **Forbidden** | Same professional-substitution theory the FTC charged in DoNotPay. Intake already outs this. Competitor copies that fail this test: DogAdvise “professional dog behaviorist right in your pocket”; Tailo “a bit like having a behaviourist watch the footage over your shoulder.” |
| “Stops reactivity” / “cures” / efficacy outcomes | **Forbidden** (until you have trials) | Outcome claim. Needs competent and reliable scientific evidence *before* the ad runs. “AI” does not lower the bar. Pfizer factors punish a false safety/efficacy claim hardest. |
| “Calmness score” / “emotion: X%” as a single number | **Risky** | Pawfessor ships a 0–100 calmness score. A number reads as measurement. Apple 1.4.1 will ask for methodology if this is framed as health. If used at all: band + confidence + “not a medical or safety score,” and never as a green light. |
| “We see [signal]; likely [state] (confidence); try [1–3 actions]; stop if [X]” | **Allowed-if-qualified** | This is the intake output shape and the only honest one. Qualifications that must be *on the same screen*: observable signals only; “likely,” never “is”; numeric or low/med/high confidence; actions limited to the next ~60 seconds; hard-stop line visible; “not a diagnosis; not a safety clearance; does not replace a veterinarian or DACVB.” Refuse the card entirely below the confidence floor or on bite-risk / medical / kids-in-frame. |
| “Helps you notice body-language signals and pick a next step” | **Allowed-if-qualified** | Education / competence framing. Still needs the hard-stop and the “not a vet / not a translator” line in listing copy, not only in the ToS. |
| “On-device” / “never leaves your phone” / “private — we don’t upload” | **Forbidden** on v1 | **[F] CFO lock.** Each read is a cloud multimodal call. FTC deception: an on-device privacy claim is false the moment the clip leaves the device. Apple 5.1.2 requires a separate explicit permission before sharing with third-party AI. Purpose string + pre-clip screen must say the video is sent to our processors. On-device is only allowed if founder later forces that architecture and it is actually true. |
