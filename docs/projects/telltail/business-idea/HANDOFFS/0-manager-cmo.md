# Manager brief — Telltail — Phase 0 (CMO peer)

## Operator brief (plain English)

The “AI dog translator” lane is already a public joke and a trainer-safety scare — we inherit that tax the second we sound like one. Competence (what to do in the next 60 seconds, plus a hard stop) is the only honest wedge; empathy is what every live competitor already sells. **Telltail** the name is mixed, leaning hurts: linguistically it reads as telltale signs, but `telltail.com` is a sunset puppy-matching brand, a live USPTO mark sits on pet adoption/breeding, and **Telltail Dog Training** (Little Rock, Elizabeth Silverstein) is a live competence-coded trainer. Keep the locked name, never buy “translator” traffic, and put a competence subtitle on the name so it does not get heard as a talking-tail toy. **Founder lock (2026-08-21): Telltail is an AI dog trainer / training tool, not a toy.** Sit next to Pupford/Zigzag as a trainer, never next to Traini as a sticker. Phase 1 is CEO’s. Phase 0 is not approved from this seat.

## Positioning — competence vs empathy

> Simulated council (Dunford / Schwartz / Sharp as dissenter) — frameworks only, not a real review.

- **Dunford:** alternatives are (a) translator apps, (b) YouTube / generic courses, (c) a booked trainer. We beat (a) by refusing the toy job, (b) by being *this moment*, (c) by being the 60-second bridge and the escalate, not a replacement.
- **Schwartz:** sophistication is late. “Better AI translator” is a burned claim. The next claim has to be a new mechanism (instruction + stop-rule), not a louder accuracy %.
- **Sharp (dissenter):** “dog translator” is the phrase people can say. Refusing it costs mental availability. **Chair override:** buying that phrase trains us as the thing trainers already called dangerous. We will grow slower on problem language rather than faster as a gag.

**Buying psychology applied**

| Playbook | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| JTBD | Job: “When I don’t know why he did that, help me do the next right thing so I don’t make it worse.” Not “talk to my dog.” |
| Means-end | Vision read → 1–3 next actions → I stay capable / the dog doesn’t escalate. Feature never leads. |
| Loss aversion | Hero the avoided loss (wrong hug, flooded fear, kid at the mouth). Stronger than “deeper bond.” |
| Cialdini / authority | Named trainer or behaviorist on the box. No fake “94%.” Reciprocity asset = free *observable signals* checklist, not a translator teaser. |
| Fogg | Ability cost of the first read must be one clip, one screen, three actions. Prompt = the scary moment, not a daily streak toy. |

**Working positioning line (assumption — not locked copy):**  
*See the signal. Do the next right thing — and know when to stop.*

**Pillars**

| Pillar | Claim | Proof we must have | Guardrail |
|--------|-------|--------------------|-----------|
| Competence | What to do in the next 60 seconds | 1–3 actions, not a mood sticker | Do not lead with “understands your dog’s feelings” |
| Honesty | Confidence + what would change the read | Visible uncertainty | No accuracy %; no cartoon quotes |
| Safety | Hard stop to vet / certified behaviorist | Escalate on bite-risk, pain, freeze+stare | Do not sell self-serve rehab |
| Authority | A real training voice | Named expert, method we will stand behind | No anonymous “PetGPT” |
| Category | **AI dog trainer / training tool** (founder lock) | Store/ads/PR word ban on “translator”; sit with Pupford/Zigzag | Never Traini/sticker comps. Do not echo TellTail the breeder marketplace |

## Offer lock (CFO → CMO, 2026-08-21)

| Tier | What we can say | What we must not say |
|------|-----------------|----------------------|
| **Free** | 3–5 Lite reads. Enough to handle *one real scare* and see the stop-rule. Reciprocity, not a tease that fails mid-moment. | “Full AI.” Frontier model. Unlimited try. |
| **Plus $9–13/mo** | Metered Flash-class. Working default **40 included reads**. Sit next to Pupford/Zigzag as *40 moments that matter*, not as an unlimited course library. | “Unlimited reads.” “Unlimited AI coach.” Frontier-every-time. |
| **Frontier / unmetered** | Not a Plus SKU. Dies on COGS if sold at coffee-tier. | Any $9–13 hero that implies unmetered multimodal. |

**Why the meter helps brand. [I]** A cap kills the translator binge (“make it talk 200 times”). It matches competence: you open the app when it matters.

**Why the meter hurts vs Pupford. [I]** Pupford/Zigzag sell unlimited *curriculum*. A bad puppy week can burn 40 reads. Do not comparison-ad “same price, unlimited help.” Comparison is *this moment, this dog* vs a video library — different job, same wallet.

**Conversion / Fogg:** first Lite read must complete (clip → 3 actions → stop-rule) or the free tier trains refunds. Credits leftover is a feature, not a guilt trip.
