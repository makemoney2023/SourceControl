# Manager brief — Telltail — Phase 0 (Head of Research peer brief)

## Operator brief (plain English)

The named “AI dog translator / trainer” set is live but not one category: four vision products already sell body-language reads, Traini is a funded translator-plus-lessons hybrid, Dogly is an expert daily plan (not camera), and “Dog AI” is not a brand. The instruction *job* is real — UK Dogs Trust 2024 shows owners over-confident, already Googling “what do I do,” and almost never in class — but the *phone-camera, next-60-seconds* product form is still a hoped-for escape from a 24-year toy/translator lineage. Closest on-wedge comps are Pawfessor (iOS clip + tips), Tailo (Android/web “what to try next”), and PetSignalAI (web escalate gates). No TAM. Most vision apps have zero public ratings; **Traini is the exception** (US App Store **3.9 / 295 ratings**; Play **5M+** download *bucket* — not MAU). This peer brief is on disk. Orchestrator: Phase 0 stays closed; this seat does not reopen it.

## What we found

- **Set verified (fact, 2026-08-21 pages, Set B pass folded in):** KEEP vision+instruction — Pawfessor, Tailo, PetSignalAI, Como (claims), Dog Advise (Play, 50+), Pawlyze, Aplexity *AI Dog Translator & Scanner*, LunaDogAI, PN1 hybrid. PARK as sticker-with-volume — **Traini** (real store: 3.9/295 iOS; Play 5M+ bucket). DROP — dogly.com from vision set (expert plan, $9/$19). DROP generic “Dog AI.” Accelar’s *other* Dogly (`dogly.accelar.io`) claims camera+academy at $4.99/mo but **no App Store ID found** — vaporware-risk, not a KEEP.
- **Demand split (fact + inference):** Toy/translator demand is old and crowded (BowLingual 2002 → 2026 Entertainment-category “AI Dog Translator” apps + PettiChat collar). Instruction demand is independently real (Dogs Trust NDS 2024; paid Zigzag/GoodPup moment-help; ChatGPT-as-budget-trainer quote). Vision-app *capture* of that instruction job is unproven — instruction-framed apps exist and charge, but **almost no ratings**. The one app with volume (Traini) is sticker-first and already trainer-attacked.
- **Instruction wedge (inference, medium confidence):** The *job* is real. The *form* (LLM vision → 1–3 actions in 60 seconds) is not yet a paid category with public proof. Hybrid listings (PN1 “What to Do” + “Dog Quote”; Traini translator + 200 lessons) show how easy it is to slide back into stickers.
- **Science risk (fact):** Martvel et al., *Sci Rep* 21 Nov 2025 — zero-shot GPT/Gemini/LLaVA look moderate on layperson-labeled web photos, then drop to near chance on experimentally elicited Labrador faces; backgrounds drive labels. Authors: current LVLMs “are not yet suitable for reliable, biologically grounded recognition of canine emotions.” Pawfessor and Tailo both disclose Gemini as the video backend.
- **Critique source (fact, corrected):** Intake’s Adopt-a-Pet Traini critique **is real** — it is the **same** Marianne Eloise article as Kinship, 16 Apr 2026, two URLs (`adoptapet.com/blog/lifestyle/can-traini-really-translate-your-dogs-emotions` and `kinship.com/dog-lifestyle/…`). Not two independent critiques. Quotes verified on both pages (Easterbrook / Lawley-Rudd / Grossman).
- **CFO lock absorbed 2026-08-21 (fact from Orchestrator/CFO):** product is a **multimodal LLM read per clip**, not a custom dog-pose detector. Price band **$9–13/mo only works metered** (Plus cap **60** Flash-class reads/mo; was 40). Packaging lock 2026-08-21. Sci Rep risk is now a locked-stack fact, not a “maybe we train a pose model” fork.

## Instruction vs toy (demand)

**Toy job — high confidence it exists and people pay for it.** [F] Takara BowLingual (2002) sold as entertainment (BBC 27 Sep 2002; Ig Nobel). 2026 App Store is a thicket of near-duplicate “AI Dog Translator” titles; Iterica’s listing is filed **Entertainment** and tells the user the translations are not training or science. PettiChat (2026 collar) is the same itch in hardware; accuracy numbers in press are company claims.

**Instruction job — high confidence the job exists; low–medium that vision apps already capture paid demand.** [F] Dogs Trust National Dog Survey 2024 (373,216 owners / 430,406 dogs; PDF opened this pass):

- 80% confident reading body language; only 24% consistently identified a worried dog that needed space.
- 76% misread an appeasement roll as “wants a belly rub.”
- 76% of dogs showed ≥1 “undesirable” behaviour; <7% of owners currently in class.
- For a given problem, 31–42% searched the internet.
- 78% believed their own dog would never bite them.

[F] People already pay (or substitute) for *moment-level human* instruction: Zigzag Premium listed £9.99/mo or £39.99/yr for 24/7 trainer/behaviourist access (company page); GoodPup ~$29–34/week + 24/7 chat (Rover review + marketing); trainer Jessica Logan quotes a client “I’ll use ChatGPT for now. I’m on a budget.” Those are **not** vision-app revenues.

[I] Telltail’s only structural advantage vs ChatGPT is the camera. If the camera is wrong (Sci Rep), that advantage is a liability — the same false-confident “relaxed” failure the intake already flagged.

**Wedge verdict:** Do not build a better BowLingual. The paid analogues to study in Phase 2 are Zigzag chat, GoodPup, LunaDogAI, PetSignalAI’s $4.99 episode, and “I’ll use ChatGPT” — not bark-translator review scores. **Confidence: medium** on the job; **medium-low** on vision-app monetization of that job.

No TAM. No search volumes. One blog’s “22,200 monthly searches” for `dog translator` was seen and **discarded** (not a tool export). Como’s $261B / 500M ticker unused.
