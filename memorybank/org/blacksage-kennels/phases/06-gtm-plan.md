## Executive summary

Blacksage Kennels goes to market as a **trust-first, organic, selective-placement kennel**. Serious ADRK-aligned buyers discover Blacksage through search, trainers, clubs, and word-of-mouth; they **shortlist and verify on the owned website**; then they **Begin your inquiry** into Interest list (Package A) → Waitlist (Package B) → Placement (Package C). Prestige = **evidence density**. Paid acquisition is **skipped** unless the operator explicitly funds Phase 19. No FOMO puppy ads. No price-led acquisition. Launch follows PRD tiers **0 → 1 → 2**.

**Three big bets**

1. **Owned web as primary demand surface** — site earns shortlist survival; every other channel funnels here.  
2. **Education before inquiry (D2)** — Health/Education + Dogs (or honest empty state) before promoting conversion.  
3. **Referrer shareability over volume** — one trainer willing to share the URL beats a thousand impulse form fills.

## 1. Channels

### Channel architecture (ORB)

| Type | Priority | Role | Status |
|------|----------|------|--------|
| **Owned** | Primary | Website + interest/inquiry email | **Active in plan** |
| **Borrowed** | Supporting | Referrers, clubs, trainers, show/working networks | **Active — relationship-led** |
| **Rented** | Secondary | Facebook and/or Instagram (max 1–2) | **Optional** — skip if no operator capacity |
| **Paid** | — | Google Ads, boosted puppy ads, marketplaces | **SKIPPED** (Phase 19 deferred) |

**Rule:** Everything rented or borrowed **funnels to owned**. Platform presence never substitutes for site proof.

### Owned (primary)

| Asset | Role | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| **Website** | Shortlist + verify; primary conversion surface | IA: Home → Dogs → Health/Education → About → Contact/Inquire |
| **Interest / inquiry email** | Package A nurture; Tier 2 upgrade notices | Low frequency, high value — no weekly puppy spam |
| **Inquiry auto-reply** | Sets review expectations | Requires Q7 destination before public launch |

### Borrowed (supporting — PR-led)

| Channel | Role | Gate |
|---------|------|------|
| Trainer referrals | Highest-quality discovery | Tier 1 site must pass M5 sniff test |
| Breed club / ARC-adjacent education | Credibility adjacency | Soft intro; no kennel ads in forums |
| Working / sport / show contacts | Sub-segment discovery | Tier 2 when titles/working narrative verified |
| Prior owner WOM | Social proof | Post-placement only; verified testimonials |

**Category orgs (context only — no assumed Blacksage membership):** AMRRC, AKC responsible-breeder guidance, OFA/CHIC. Club affiliations are Tier 2 claims until operator documents them.

### Rented (optional, capped)

| Platform | Use | Avoid |
|----------|-----|-------|
| **Facebook** (preferred first if any) | Education posts → site Health/Education | Boosted puppy ads; price; availability spam |
| **Instagram** | Real operator media when Q6 satisfied → link in bio | Stock dogs; FOMO stories; “DM for price” |

**Recommendation:** Start with **one** platform where the operator already has presence — or **skip rented at launch**. Owned + borrowed is enough for bootstrapped GTM.

### Explicitly skipped / never

| Channel | Verdict |
|---------|---------|
| Google Ads / paid social | Deferred Phase 19 |
| Puppy marketplaces (Lancaster-class) | **Never** — anti-persona magnet |
| Press newswire / paid PR retainer | Skip — no mass splash before proof |
| Referral commissions / affiliate codes | Skip v1 — transactional in this category |
| Guard-dog / protection co-marketing | Never — ADRK temperament bounds |

### ICP × channel fit (summary)

| Audience | Best discover paths | Message emphasis | Do not |
|----------|---------------------|------------------|--------|
| Family companion | Search + trainer referral → site | Temperament, socialization, education | Guard-dog tropes; available-now posts |
| Sport / working | Club/sport networks → Dogs + Health | Working aptitude within standard | Aggression / machismo marketing |
| Import / pedigree | Pedigree research → Dogs + standard literacy | Verifiable pedigrees, natural tail | “Rare bloodline” without proof |
| Referrer | Direct relationship → shareable URL | Evidence-dense site they can stake name on | Ask to share before M5 pass |
| Anti-persona | Marketplaces, price SEO, FOMO ads | — | Do not serve these channels |

## 2. Demand path — Interest → Waitlist → Placement

### Buyer journey

```
DISCOVER → SHORTLIST → VERIFY (on-site) → BEGIN YOUR INQUIRY → QUALIFY (off-site)
                              ↑
                    Site GTM job completes here
         → Package A (Interest) / B (Waitlist) / C (Placement)
```

| Stage | Buyer action | Blacksage surface | GTM action |
|-------|--------------|-------------------|------------|
| **Discover** | Search, referral, optional social | Borrowed/rented → **owned site** | SEO education; referrer URL; optional 1 rented platform |
| **Shortlist** | 3–8 site scan | **Home** | Program identity + proof pathway — not apply-first |
| **Verify** | Dogs, Health/Education, About | Trust IA | Tier 1 education; Tier 2 named stock + registry links |
| **Inquire** | Form after proof | **Contact/Inquire** | CTA: **Begin your inquiry** / Join interest list |
| **Qualify** | Phone/email | Off-site | Operator-owned; site sets expectations |
| **Package** | Match by maturity + fit | Off-site process | A → B → C sequencing |

### Packaging map (PRD-aligned)

| Package | Trigger (Q1) | Site UX / CTA | Payment |
|---------|--------------|---------------|---------|
| **A — Interest list** | Pre-litter / brand-first (Tier 1) | Join our interest list | None |
| **B — Waitlist** | Active program (Tier 2) | Begin your inquiry → waitlist fields | None on site; deposit off-site after approval |
| **C — Placement** | Waitlist match + contract | Process description only | Off-site |

**Monetization sequencing (never invert):**

```
Trust content → Inquiry → Qualification → Price discussion (off-site) → Deposit (if waitlisted) → Placement
```

### Tier paths

**Tier 1:** Referrer/Google → Home → Health/Education → About → Interest list (A); Dogs = honest empty state  

**Tier 2:** Referrer/Google → Home → Dogs → Health/Education → About → Inquiry (A or B) → operator qualifies → B waitlist or C placement

## 3. Launch outline (Tier 0 → 1 → 2)

*Scorecard-required. Aligns to PRD staged launch.*

### Narrative (one story, three stages)

Blacksage introduces itself as an **ADRK-aligned Rottweiler program that earns trust through evidence and education, then invites qualified inquiry.** The story does not change between tiers — only **what we can show** changes. Never invent dogs, litters, geography, titles, health results, or prices.

### Tier 0 — Staging (internal)

| Element | Plan |
|---------|------|
| **Audience** | Operator, build, QA — not public |
| **PR / promotion** | **None** |
| **Content** | Tier 1 copy drafts; E1–E8 education; claim audit |
| **Exit** | PRD LG1–LG7 / `AC-GATE-001`; Q7 plan; M5 sniff test planned |

### Tier 1 — Brand-first (Q1 = pre-litter / coming-soon)

| Element | Plan |
|---------|------|
| **Live sections** | Home, About, Health/Education, Contact/Interest list |
| **Dogs** | Honest empty / coming-soon — no fake profiles |
| **Conversion** | Package A only |
| **Owned launch** | Soft site live + interest-list welcome expectations |
| **Borrowed** | Selective 1:1 referrer sniff test (2–3 trusted contacts) before wider share |
| **Rented** | Optional one education post → Health/Education; or skip |
| **PR posture** | Quiet credibility — no press release, no directory “active breeding” claims |
| **Do not** | Puppy availability posts, paid ads, FOMO, stock dog photos as proof |

**Sample framing (not final copy):**  
*Blacksage Kennels breeds German / ADRK-aligned Rottweilers toward FCI Standard No. 147. Explore health and education resources, learn our placement philosophy, and — when ready — join our interest list or begin your inquiry.*

### Tier 2 — Active program (Q1 = active + inventory)

| Element | Plan |
|---------|------|
| **Live sections** | Full IA including populated Dogs (+ Litters when verified) |
| **Conversion** | Package A and/or B; C in process copy only |
| **Owned moments** | Breeding-stock profiles live → email interest list; waitlist inquiry open; litter pages only with verified facts |
| **Borrowed** | Expand trainer/club 1:1 outreach; deep links to Dogs + Health |
| **Rented** | Optional real-media updates → site; still no price / “X left” |
| **PR posture** | Controlled announcement when dogs + health links verified; earned media optional/low priority |
| **Deposit copy** | “May be required after approval. Terms provided individually.” — **no amounts** |

### Launch checklist (both public tiers)

- [ ] Zero Tier 3 claims (prices, fake dogs, invented memberships)  
- [ ] CTA locks: Begin your inquiry / Interest list / waitlist consideration  
- [ ] Q7 inquiry destination live  
- [ ] M5: ≥1 referrer would share URL without caveat (or Tier 1 honest posture accepted)  
- [ ] E1–E8 education substantive before inquire is promoted  
- [ ] No FOMO or price-led posts issued  

### Tier promotion

```
Tier 0 → Tier 1: Tier 1 pages + NFR + zero Tier 3 + Q7 documented
Tier 1 → Tier 2: Q1 active + health inventory + Q6 photos + Dogs populated + operator sign-off
```

## 5. Reputation, community, and referral PR

### Reputation principle

In this category, **reputation is evidence, not narrative.** Claim discipline (SD5) is the PR strategy. Referrer shareability (M5) is the north-star PR outcome — not press hits.

**Earn:** Owned proof IA, health transparency, honest posture, shareable URL, soft network presence.  
**Protect:** No Tier 3 claims, no placeholder dogs as proof, no apply-first/price UX, no aggression marketing.

### Soft intro vs hard pitch

| Default (Tier 1) | Only when Tier 2 proof exists |
|------------------|-------------------------------|
| Soft intro: education + honest program stage | Ask for explicit referral endorsement |
| Share URL when asked “what are you building?” | Deep links to Dogs + Health |
| Contribute value in communities first | Club education contribution with verified content |

**Never:** Puppy availability posts in forums; “PM for price”; guard-dog marketing; astroturf; invented memberships; spam self-promo.

### Referral loop (organic)

```
Positive trigger (placement / trainer satisfaction)
  → Operator 1:1 ask (not mass FOMO email)
  → Referrer shares canonical URL
  → Buyer verifies on site → inquiry
  → “How did you hear about us?” → thank referrer on qualified leads only
```

No affiliate/commission program in Phase 6 base case.

### Co-marketing priorities (types, not invented partners)

1. Rottweiler-experienced trainers  
2. Breed club education channels  
3. Working/sport orgs (Tier 2)  
4. Vets — local after Q2  
5. Prior owners (when exist)  
6. Show network (Tier 2)  
7. Breed-health education platforms  
8. Local pet press — optional Tier 2 only  

Geography-specific execution **deferred until Q2**.

### Crisis anti-patterns (do not execute)

Puppy forum ads · price in any channel · FOMO timers · guard-dog language · fake memberships · stock photos as Blacksage dogs · newswire splash · paid influencers · marketplace listings · deposits before qualification · arguing with trolls in public.

**Response principles:** Fix factual errors within 24h; operator voice; point to published education; document for M7 audit.

## 6. Messaging and CTA locks

**Core message:** Blacksage Kennels is a German / ADRK-aligned Rottweiler breeding program. We lead with verifiable evidence and standards-informed education — then invite qualified inquiry. Placements are selective and mutual.

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|-------|
| **Begin your inquiry** | Apply now / Get your puppy |
| **Join our interest list** | Reserve your spot / Sign up now |
| **Submit inquiry for waitlist consideration** | Apply now (checkout urgency) |
| View our dogs / Health & testing / Learn our process | Buy / Shop / Reserve / Available now |
| Pricing discussed after qualification | Starting at $X / How much? |
| Inquiry is not a reservation | Only X left / Limited time |
| Breeding program in development (Tier 1) | Available puppies / Ready to go home |

**Voice:** Confident · Calm · Precise · Evidence-led · Respectful.

## 7. Measurement and success

### North stars

| ID | Metric | Target posture |
|----|--------|----------------|
| **M4** | Qualified inquiries | Quality > volume |
| **M5** | Referrer shareability | ≥1 referrer shares URL without caveat |
| **M6** | Shortlist + verify on-site | Pass Phase 2 trust heuristic |
| **M2/M7** | Claim discipline | 100% Tier 1–2 only |
| **PR-M3** | Reputation incidents | **0** |

### Leading indicators

- Time on Health/Education; Health → Contact path  
- Inquiry quality tags (qualified / neutral / anti-persona)  
- Inquire preceded by trust-page sessions (not landing on form)  
- “How did you hear about us?” mix (referral + search + club)  
- Interest list → waitlist conversion when Tier 2 opens  

### Not success

Raw traffic · form volume alone · follower counts · viral puppy content · price-inquiry volume · press-hit count / AVE.

### 90-day launch checklist

- [ ] Site live at agreed tier; zero Tier 3 claims  
- [ ] M5 referrer shareability confirmed  
- [ ] First inquiries majority qualified  
- [ ] No anti-persona surge from bad language  
- [ ] Q7 routing works  
- [ ] ≥1 education asset indexed  
- [ ] Zero FOMO / price-led posts
