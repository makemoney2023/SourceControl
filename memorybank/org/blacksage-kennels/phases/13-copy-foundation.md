## 1. Brand voice

*Aligned to `11-brand-system.md` §5 and `.agents/product-marketing.md`.*

### Personality

**Confident · Calm · Precise · Evidence-led · Respectful**

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| Confident | Arrogant or macho |
| Calm | Cold or detached |
| Precise | Jargony or clinical |
| Evidence-led | Vague superlatives without proof |
| Respectful | Elitist, shame-based, or gatekeeping |

### Tone by route

| Route | Tone shift | Example |
|-------|------------|---------|
| Home | Declarative, proof-forward | "ADRK-aligned breeding with health clearances informing every pairing." |
| Dogs | Factual, specific | "Sire — OFA hips excellent." *(Tier 2 only, when verified)* |
| Health/Education | Authoritative, accessible | "Hip and elbow evaluations are standard practice in responsible Rottweiler breeding." |
| About | Personal when operator provides; honest gaps otherwise | "[OPERATOR_STORY] or program-in-development copy when pending." |
| Inquire | Welcoming but clear | "Tell us about your home and experience. We review every inquiry individually." |

### Vocabulary

**Prefer:** structure, temperament, devoted, deliberate, inquiry, placement, standard-aligned, health clearances, verifiable, ADRK-aligned, FCI Standard No. 147, natural tail, even-tempered, biddable, self-assured

**Avoid:** fur baby, guard dog (as hype), attack, weapon, killer, 100% healthy, disease-free, rare/exotic, giant, oversized, #1, perfect family dog (generic), amazing, incredible, limited time, act now

**Breed-standard terms (use correctly):**

- "Black with clearly defined rich tan markings" — not "tri-color" or "brown patches"
- "Compact, powerful build" — not "massive" or "XL"
- "Natural tail" (German/ADRK context) — not "docked" as selling point
- FCI temperament: good-natured, placid, devoted, obedient, eager to work, self-assured, even-tempered

### Sentence rhythm

- **Headlines (h1/h2):** Short. Often two beats. 4–10 words for page heroes; 3–6 words for proof cell titles.
- **Subheads:** 12–22 words max. One idea.
- **Body (education):** Mix short declarative with one explanatory sentence. Max ~22 words per sentence. Max width 68ch.
- **Lists:** Three items preferred; five max without subheads.
- **Paragraphs:** 2–3 sentences on marketing pages; single-sentence allowed for emphasis.

### Reading level

Grade 8–10 (Flesch-Kincaid). Breed-standard language allowed when paired with plain-English gloss.

### Voice examples

#### Good

> German / ADRK-aligned Rottweilers. We lead with health transparency and standards-informed education — then invite qualified inquiry.

> Health clearances inform every pairing. When available, results link to OFA and related registries for independent verification.

> Breeding stock profiles are coming soon. In the meantime, explore our health and education resources.

> Tell us about your home and experience. We review every inquiry individually.

#### Bad

> 🐾 AMAZING GUARD DOGS!!! Protect your family NOW — limited puppies!!!

> All our dogs are 100% healthy and disease-free — best lines in the country!

> Born of German standard. Built to standard. Steady by nature. *(v1 scroll beats — rejected as IA)*

> Snag your dream pup before they're gone! Apply today!!!

Pillars map to **pages and proof modules**, not scroll depth. Each route leads with its primary pillar(s); cross-links compound trust across the site.

|--------|---------|----------------|------------------|

```
  All 4 pillars    Pillar 1+3    Pillars 1–5        Pillar 4+5   Pillar 4

```

### Claims guardrails (merged — PMM)

| Safe (Tier 1) | Avoid (Tier 3 / anti-claims) |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| "ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147 aligned breeding" | "ADRK certified" (unless affiliation verified) |
| "Health clearances inform every pairing" | "100% healthy," "guaranteed disease-free" |
| "Even-tempered, devoted, self-assured — matched to suitable homes" | "Non-aggressive," "safe with everyone always" |
| "Selective placements; inquiry reviewed individually" | "Limited time," "only X left," countdown timers |
| "Working capability and family loyalty" | "Guard dog," "protection machine," "weapon," "killer" |
| "Breeding stock profiles are coming soon" (honest empty) | Fake dog names, stock photos as program proof |

**Tier badges (brand §7.4):** `Standard reference` · `Program policy` · `Verified` · `Coming soon` — every on-page claim maps to a tier + badge. Zero Tier 3 at launch.

**Anti-persona filter (calm, never shame):** impulse shoppers, guard-dog fantasy seekers, price-only comparators, checkout-expecters.

## 3. Schwartz awareness — multi-page journey

Buyer journey across **routes**, not scroll percentage. Primary traffic: organic search, referrals, breed-club links.

| Stage | Who they are | Site routes | Copy job |
|-------|--------------|-------------|----------|
| **1. Unaware** | Considering a large breed; Rottweiler is one option | Home (positioning), Health `#standards` | Establish breed identity; ADRK/FCI frame without jargon overload |
| **2. Problem Aware** | Wants stable companion; wary of bad breeders | Health `#standards`, `#temperament` | Differentiate quality breeding; name volume/hype problem without fear-messaging |
| **3. Solution Aware** | Comparing reputable breeders; knows health testing | Health `#testing`, Dogs | Prove mechanism — how responsible programs test and pair |
| **4. Product Aware** | Found Blacksage; evaluating fit | Dogs, About, Health `#placement` | Proof stack — process, standards, selectivity |
| **5. Most Aware** | Ready to inquire | `/inquire` | Minimal re-pitch; clear expectations; mutual qualification |

### Journey map (multi-page)

```
DISCOVER (Home proof band)
    ↓
SHORTLIST (Home positioning + nav)
    ↓
VERIFY (Dogs + Health + About)
    ↓
CONTACT (/inquire)
```

| Route | Awareness served | Primary angle |
|-------|------------------|---------------|
| `/` | Unaware → Solution | Identity + proof pathway — **no primary inquire CTA above fold** |
| `/dogs` | Solution → Product | Named stock or honest empty — competence signal |
| `/health` | Problem → Product | Education + evidence density |
| `/about` | Product | Operator identity + program principles |
| `/inquire` | Product → Most Aware | Mutual qualification — not lead capture |

### Minimum trust path (content-strategist)

Buyer should have **opportunity** to verify before converting:

```
Home (proof band) → at least one of: /dogs OR /health → /inquire
```

Ideal sequence: `/` → `/health` → `/dogs` → `/about` → `/health#placement` → `/inquire`. Deep-link to `/inquire` still shows expectation copy + trust footer — never "Apply now."

**Q1 brand-first:** Litters route omitted; Dogs may be empty; Package A only. **Q1 active:** Litters when verified; Package B fields enabled.

### Messaging rules by stage

| Stage | Do | Don't |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Unaware–Problem | Educate on German type and temperament truth | Sell puppies; use scarcity |
| Solution | Show mechanism (health categories, pairing philosophy) | Generic "quality" without specifics |
| Product | Proof (process, standards, selectivity) | Re-introduce brand from zero on every page |
| Most Aware | Short path to inquiry | Long re-convincing on `/inquire` |

**v1 rejected:** Scroll region → awareness mapping (Hero 0–25%, Heritage 25–56%, etc.). **Do not restore.**

## 8. Do / Don't

### Do

- Lead Home with **proof summary band** — four cells above fold
- Route inquiry to **`/inquire`** with CTA **Begin your inquiry**
- Use **evidence-led** copy before any conversion push
- Write **honest empty states** for Dogs and About
- Use ADRK/FCI language **accurately**
- Describe dogs as **self-assured, even-tempered, good-natured**
- Frame inquiry as **deliberate placement**, not purchase
- Contrast **selectivity vs volume** without attacking competitors
- Support **Packages A–C** with distinct language
- Omit per-dog health rows when data absent — never fake proof

### Don't — anti-aggression / anti-FOMO

| Anti-pattern | Why |
|--------------|-----|
| "Guard dog," "protection dog," "personal protection" as hero copy | Machismo / intimidation marketing |
| "Aggressive," "killer drive," "beast," "weapon" | Aggression tropes |
| Spiked-collar / bared-teeth imagery language | Visual aggression cue |
| "Fur babies," excessive emoji | Undermines precise voice |
| "Imported European" without specifics | Faux-European cliché |
| "Rare," "exotic," "XL," "massive" | Trend breeding language |
| Puppy-mill urgency ("only 2 left," "going fast," countdown) | Violates selectivity pillar |
| Scroll narrative / heritage scroll H2 system | v1 rejected IA |
| `/apply` route or "Apply now" button | Contradicts D2 + Phase 12 |
| Tagline replacing proof sections | Evidence-led credibility first |
| Invented OFA results, dog photos, litter dates | Tier 3 violation |
| On-site prices, deposit amounts | A10 lock |

## 10. Breed copy anchors

Source: FCI Standard No. 147 (ADRK-aligned). Use factually — not as filler.

### Origin and standard

- **Origin:** Germany
- **Standard:** FCI Standard No. 147 (Rottweiler)
- **Origin club:** ADRK
- **Copy usage:** "ADRK-aligned" / "ADRK / FCI Standard No. 147 aligned" — not "certified by ADRK" unless operator holds credentials

### Temperament (FCI-aligned)

> Good-natured, placid in basic disposition, and devoted to its owner. Obedient, eager to work, and self-assured. Fearless and even-tempered — not nervous or aggressive.

**Plain-English gloss:** Calm baseline, devoted to family, confident without being reactive — willing to work when asked.

### Appearance

- **Coat:** Black with clearly defined rich tan markings
- **Build:** Compact and powerful
- **Tail:** Natural (undocked) under German/ADRK context

### Size (education — state once on `/health#standards`)

|-----|--------|--------|
| Males | 61–68 cm | ~50 kg |
| Females | 56–63 cm | ~42 kg |
