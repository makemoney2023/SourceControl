## A. Positioning lock for copy

### One-line

**Power with nobility.** ADRK-minded German Rottweilers — bred for structure, steadiness, and devotion.

### Positioning statement

Blacksage Kennels breeds German-type Rottweilers aligned with ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147: black dogs with clearly defined rich tan markings, compact powerful build, natural tail, and a temperament that is good-natured, placid, devoted, and self-assured. We serve experienced owners and families who research health, structure, and breeding standards before they inquire — not impulse buyers seeking a status symbol or guard-dog fantasy.

### Differentiation — what we are / are not

| We are | We are not |
|--------|------------|
| A selective, ADRK-aligned German-type Rottweiler program | A volume puppy mill or "always available" kennel |
| Deliberate about structure, health testing, and temperament | A hype guard-dog brand (machismo, aggression marketing) |
| A trust-first brand experience that earns inquiry | A checkout-first pet shop ("Buy now," "Reserve your pup") |
| Honest about breed capability — companion, family, working heritage | A generic "perfect family dog for everyone" promise |
| Premium in presentation and placement standards | Elitist or dismissive toward sincere buyers |

### Claims we can make (proof-shaped)

Use factual, verifiable framing. Tie claims to process and standard — not superlatives.

| Claim theme | Safe copy direction | Proof anchor |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------|
| **Breed alignment** | "ADRK/FCI Standard No. 147 aligned breeding" | FCI standard; ADRK as origin club (MEMORY/context.md) |
| **German type** | "German-type Rottweilers with clearly defined rich tan markings" | Standard appearance language |
| **Temperament truth** | "Good-natured, devoted, self-assured — bred for steadiness, not spectacle" | ADRK temperament descriptors |
| **Structure focus** | "Correct type and deliberate pairing inform every breeding decision" | Process language; photos when available |
| **Health approach** | "Health clearances and temperament assessment inform every pairing" | Process — list specific tests when operator confirms |
| **Selectivity** | "Placements are deliberate; we review every inquiry" | Application flow; waitlist reality |
| **Heritage** | "German origin, working heritage, family devotion" | Breed history; no faux-European clichés |
| **Experience** | "Built for owners who research before they inquire" | Tone + application gate |

### Claims to avoid (unverifiable or off-brand)

| Avoid | Why | Say instead |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| "100% healthy," "guaranteed no issues," "disease-free" | Unverifiable; legal/regulatory risk | "Health clearances inform our pairings" |
| "Best Rottweilers in [region/world]" | Unsubstantiated superlative | "Deliberately bred German-type Rottweilers" |
| "Non-aggressive," "harmless," "safe with everyone always" | Overpromises; ignores responsible ownership | "Even-tempered, devoted, self-assured — matched to suitable homes" |
| "Rare," "exotic," "unique bloodline" (unless documented) | Status-seeking bait | "ADRK-minded type and structure" |
| "Limited time," "only X puppies left," urgency countdown | Puppy-mill conversion pattern | "Selective placements; inquiry reviewed individually" |
| "Guard dog," "protection machine," "weapon," "killer" | Machismo trope; attracts wrong buyer | "Working capability and family loyalty" |
| "Perfect for first-time owners" (blanket) | Misaligned with breed + selectivity | "Tell us about your experience; we review fit" |
| Champion titles, specific test results, pricing | Until operator confirms on site | Placeholder or process-only language |
| Invented kennel photos or fake litters | Trust destroyer | Placeholder: "Photography coming soon" |

### Audience segments + jobs-to-be-done (inquire/apply)

| Segment | Job to be done | What they need to hear before applying | Apply motivation |
|---------|----------------|----------------------------------------|------------------|
| **Experienced Rottie owner** | Next dog with better structure/temperament than last source | Standards literacy rewarded; no dumbing down | Continuity of type; breeder who speaks their language |
| **Working / sport-adjacent handler** | Stable nerve, biddability, correct build for training path | Working heritage without aggression hype | Confidence in temperament screening + structure |
| **Research-heavy family** | Loyal guardian companion that fits an active household | Steadiness + devotion; honest breed expectations | Reassurance on placement process, not "easy puppy" |
| **Referral / club-adjacent buyer** | Validate kennel credibility before reaching out | ADRK alignment, selective placement, calm brand proof | Low-friction inquiry after trust sections |
| **Upgrade buyer** (pet → serious program) | Escape backyard-breeder regret | Transparent process; education-first narrative | Application as signal of seriousness |

**Anti-persona (gently deter):** impulse/status buyer, guard-dog fantasy seeker, price-shopper expecting instant availability, buyer unwilling to describe household/experience.

### Language that qualifies serious buyers (and gently deters others)

**Invite:**

- "Tell us about your home, experience, and what you're looking for."
- "We welcome inquiries from owners who've researched the breed."
- "Begin your inquiry — we review each submission."

**Filter (calm, not condescending):**

- "Our dogs are powerful, devoted companions — not status symbols."
- "We place by fit, not by first payment."
- "If you're looking for instant availability or guard-dog hype, we may not be the right kennel."
- "An application helps us understand your household — not every inquiry receives a puppy."

**Never use:** shame, elitism, "you probably can't handle," or macho gatekeeping.

## C. Objection handling for inquire/apply

| Objection | Response angle | Where on site |
|-----------|----------------|---------------|
| **"I don't know what a puppy costs / is it worth it?"** | Reframe from price-shopping to lifetime investment: selective breeding, health testing, temperament matching, support of a deliberate program — not a commodity purchase. Avoid quoting price until operator sets policy; invite conversation through inquiry. | `#trust` (value framing); `/apply` subhead: "Investment and placement details discussed after review" |
| **"The waitlist sounds long — why bother?"** | Waitlist = deliberate breeding, not inventory backlog. Quality placements beat instant checkout. Application starts conversation; not all inquiries enter waitlist immediately. | `#trust`; Apply CTA band: "Inquiry is the first step — not a commitment to purchase" |
| **"Are these aggressive dogs?"** | ADRK truth: good-natured, even-tempered, self-assured — not fearful, not sharp for spectacle. Power ≠ aggression. Responsible ownership required. | `#temperament`; `#heritage` (breed fact block); `/apply` helper near experience field |
| **"How do I know you're not a backyard breeder?"** | Contrast signals: ADRK/FCI alignment language, health testing process, temperament evaluation, selective placement, professional brand, application vetting — vs. always-available puppies and hype. | `#heritage`; `#structure`; `#trust`; `/apply` trust footer |
| **"Why apply instead of buy/reserve now?"** | Application = mutual respect and fit check. Protects dogs and buyers. Earned placement, not cart checkout. | Apply CTA band; `/apply` headline + intro paragraph; `#apply` anchor |
| **"What health testing do you actually do?"** | Commit to transparency: name test categories when operator confirms; until then — "Health clearances inform every pairing" + offer to discuss specifics during review. Never invent titles or results. | `#structure`; `#trust`; `/apply` trust footer (expandable FAQ-style helper optional) |
| **"Will this dog work for family or only working homes?"** | Dual truth: devoted family companion *and* working heritage — suitability depends on household preparation, not binary marketing. Application helps assess fit. | `#temperament`; `/apply` household description helper text |
| **"I just want to ask a quick question first."** | Inquiry form is the structured channel; short questions welcome in application. Sets expectation for response time. | `/apply` subhead; footer contact when operator email live |
| **"I've never owned a Rottweiler."** | Not auto-disqualify — ask for related experience (large breed, training, lifestyle). Honest about breed needs. | `/apply` experience field helper: "Include any large-breed or training experience" |

## D. Conversion narrative

### Why Apply is the right CTA (not Buy / Shop / Reserve)

| Buy/Shop/Reserve framing | Apply framing |
|--------------------------|---------------|
| Implies inventory and instant transaction | Implies mutual evaluation and fit |
| Attracts impulse and status buyers | Attracts researchers and prepared owners |
| Conflicts with selective, low-volume reality | Matches deliberate placement model |
| Commodity pet-market language | Premium breeder relationship language |

**Primary CTA lock:** "Begin your inquiry" — not "Apply now," "Get your puppy," "Join waitlist" (waitlist can appear in body copy, not button hype).

**Secondary patterns:** Nav label "Apply"; confirmation "Inquiry received" — never "Application approved" or "You're in!"

### Messaging that makes application feel earned and respectful

**Narrative arc (maps to landing scroll):**

1. **Arrival (Hero)** — Brand promise only. No CTA. Earn attention.
2. **Heritage** — Credibility: German origin, ADRK alignment, breed truth.
3. **Structure** — Rational proof: type, health approach, standards literacy.
4. **Temperament** — Emotional proof: devotion, steadiness, family/working duality.
5. **Trust** — Process proof: testing, evaluation, selective placement.
6. **Apply** — Invitation: you've seen how we work; inquiry is the respectful next step.

**Apply band copy skeleton:**

- **Headline:** Begin your inquiry
- **Subhead:** Tell us about your home and experience. We review every submission and respond to serious inquiries.
- **Supporting (optional):** Placements are deliberate — this is not a checkout.

**Psychology (ethical):**

- **Commitment & consistency:** Scroll investment → small structured ask (application).
- **IKEA effect:** Effort in thoughtful answers increases perceived value of placement.
- **Authority:** ADRK/FCI facts + process transparency before ask.
- **Pratfall (light):** Acknowledge wait/selectivity — increases trust vs. fake abundance.
- **Anti-scarcity:** No countdown timers; selectivity is framed as care, not FOMO.
